# Growth Content — 30 Templates (Reference)
**Writing rule:** Must look good and be useful.  
**IEE:** Hit Entertainment + Education + Inspiration where they overlap.  
**Style:** Rule of 3, stairways, simplicity, repetition, polarization, bullets, active voice, minimal commas, conciseness, white space, write as you speak, vary sentence length.

---

## 1. COMPARISON / OPPOSITES (2 lines)
**Formula:** "You don't need [X]. You need [Y]."  
**Example:** You don't need a degree. You need a high-income skill.

## 2. COMPARISON / OPPOSITES (4 lines)
**Formula:** Line 1 "You don't need X." Line 2 "You need Y." Line 3 short reason. Line 4 CTA.  
**Example:** You don't need more followers. You need more engagement. Followers are vanity. Engagement is growth.

## 3. POWERFUL ONE-LINER (bold word)
**Formula:** One sentence with one **bold** concept.  
**Example:** You feel bad because your **subconscious** mind knows your unrealized potential.

## 4. POWERFUL ONE-LINER (problem reframe)
**Formula:** "There's not a single [X] that's as [bad/complex] as your mind makes you believe."  
**Example:** There's not a single problem that's as bad as your mind makes you believe it is.

## 5. POWERFUL ONE-LINER (99% stat)
**Formula:** "99% of [X] would be solved if [Y]."  
**Example:** 99% of mental health problems would be solved if schools taught stoicism.

## 6. LIST TWEETS (hook + bullets)
**Formula:** "[Hook question or statement]:" then 5–9 bullet items.  
**Example:** The best skills to learn in 2024: • Sales • Design • Writing • Speaking • Marketing • Copywriting • Emotional control

## 7. LIST TWEETS (how to X in Y time)
**Formula:** "How to [outcome] in [timeframe]:" + bullet list of habits/steps.  
**Example:** How to change your life in 6 months: • Eat healthy • Lift weights • Sleep enough • Meditate daily • Go on long walks • Write in the morning • Learn a high-income skill

## 8. HOOK–LIST–TAKEAWAY
**Formula:** [Hook]: • [Item 1] • [Item 2] • … • [Item N]. [One-line takeaway.]  
**Example:** How to win in 6 months: • Learn a skill • Share advice • Build an audience • Do free work for social proof • Leverage testimonials for paid work. Don't overcomplicate it.

## 9. HOOK–LIST–TAKEAWAY (underrated X)
**Formula:** "Underrated [X]:" + short list + "Focus on what matters." (or similar).  
**Example:** Underrated skills: • Saying no • Doing less better • Knowing what to ignore. Focus on what matters.

## 10. RULE OF THREE (parallel structure)
**Formula:** Three sentences with same structure. [Verb] to [outcome].  
**Example:** Use mornings to create. Use afternoons to learn. Use evenings to reflect.

## 11. RULE OF THREE (build to improve)
**Formula:** "[Action] to improve your [domain]." x3.  
**Example:** Lift to improve your body. Write to improve your mind. Build to improve your life.

## 12. RULE OF THREE (polarization)
**Formula:** Three groups + where they "live" (past / future / present).  
**Example:** Depressed people live in the past. Anxious people live in the future. Happy people live in the present.

## 13. 2X3 COMPARISON (before/after timeline)
**Formula:** [Time 1]: • A • B • C. [Time 2]: • D • E • F. [One-line closer.]  
**Example:** July: Restart, No clarity, No writing skills. October: 1900+ followers, 20+ coaching calls, Wrote five e-books. Things move fast when you move fast.

## 14. 2X3 COMPARISON (cool vs boring)
**Formula:** "I used to be 'cool'." • X • Y • Z. "Now I'm 'boring'." • A • B • C. [CTA.]  
**Example:** I used to be "cool": Drugs, Parties, Alcohol. Now I'm "boring": Gym, Chess, Writing. Start being boring.

## 15. THE CALLOUT (excuse → reframe)
**Formula:** "[Common excuse]." → "[Short sharp correction]."  
**Example:** "I don't have discipline." Wrong. You choose not to be disciplined.

## 16. THE CALLOUT (wish → action)
**Formula:** "I wish I [past action]." → "You'll keep saying this until you [action]. So [action]."  
**Example:** "I wish I started sooner." You'll keep saying this until you start. So start.

## 17. THE CALLOUT (label swap)
**Formula:** "I'm a [flattering label]." → "No. You're a [honest label]."  
**Example:** "I'm a perfectionist." No. You're a procrastinator.

## 18. REPETITION LIST (Study X)
**Formula:** "Study [topic]" repeated 6–9 times, one per line.  
**Example:** Study sales. Study design. Study writing. Study marketing. Study copywriting. Study human nature. Study cognitive biases.

## 19. REPETITION LIST (Replace X with Y)
**Formula:** "Replace [bad habit] with [good habit]." x5–7.  
**Example:** Replace alcohol with water. Replace gaming with writing. Replace scrolling with mindful consumption.

## 20. REPETITION LIST (word × N + punchline)
**Formula:** Same word/phrase 6–9 times, then one contrast line, then CTA.  
**Example:** Show up. Show up. Show up. … Blow up. Put in the reps.

## 21. SHORT PERSONAL STORY (vulnerability → strength)
**Formula:** "I'm not [expected]." • [Proof 1] • [Proof 2] • [Proof 3]. And I [struggle]. But I couldn't care less. [CTA.]  
**Example:** I'm not English. • I write in English • I teach in English • I record in English. And I screw up. But I couldn't care less. Stop letting your weaknesses hold you back.

## 22. SHORT PERSONAL STORY (transformation)
**Formula:** "I'm an [identity]." [Year 1]: • X • Y • Z. [Year 2]: • A • B • C. [CTA.]  
**Example:** I'm an addict. 2019: Weed, Games, Nicotine. 2023: Fitness, Writing, Business. Find better addictions.

## 23. STOP X START X (direct)
**Formula:** "Stop [X]. Start [Y]."  
**Example:** Stop telling yourself why you can't do it. Start telling yourself why you can do it.

## 24. STOP X START X (outcomes vs actions)
**Formula:** "Stop focusing on [outcomes]. Start focusing on [actions that lead there]."  
**Example:** Stop focusing on outcomes. Start focusing on the daily actions that lead to outcomes.

## 25. STOP X START X (reason why)
**Formula:** "Stop [behavior]." "[Reason / what you're really doing]."  
**Example:** Stop talking about your goals. You're collecting dopamine you haven't earned yet.

## 26. CHALLENGING COMMON (redefinition)
**Formula:** "[Common belief] doesn't mean [assumption]. It means [reframe]."  
**Example:** A degree doesn't mean you're a great thinker. A degree means you're a great memorizer.

## 27. CHALLENGING COMMON (skill over credential)
**Formula:** "You don't need [credential] if you can [skill]."  
**Example:** You don't need a degree if you can write well.

## 28. METAPHOR (X = Y because)
**Formula:** "[Concept] is like [familiar thing]. [Explanation / because]."  
**Example:** Business is like chess. It's not about always rushing forward but about always strengthening your position.

## 29. METAPHOR (humorous twist)
**Formula:** "[Concept] is like [X]. [Unexpected or funny payoff]."  
**Example:** Building a personal brand is like lifting weights. You think it'll get you girls but you end up with a bunch of dudes asking you how you got big.

## 30. METAPHOR (transformation)
**Formula:** "[Mode/phase] is like [metamorphosis]. [What others see] until [emergence]."  
**Example:** Monk mode is like a metamorphosis. People will wonder what the hell you're doing until you emerge from your cocoon.

# Authority Content — 9 Types (Reference)
**Best authority content:** Process • Strategies • Social proof • Case studies • Deep dives • Niche insights • Proof • How-to's/advice • Showing competence.  
**Format note:** Use slides/images for authority tweets when possible (revenue graphs, before/after, testimonials, frameworks).

---

## A1. PROCESS
**Formula:** [Result claim] + short disclaimers (no paid promo, no misleading screenshots). "Here are [N] steps to [achieve X] as a [small creator/niche]:" + numbered list. **Visuals:** Revenue graph, product/course screenshot, testimonials grid.  
**Example:** My course made $641 in the last 11 days. No paid promotion. No hard self-promotion. No misleading stripe payment photos. Here are 5 steps to sell a high-quality digital product as a small creator: 1. Pick 1 Transformation…

## A2. STRATEGIES
**Formula:** "[Top 1% / best] [audience]:" + bullet list of 8–13 tactics (concise, actionable).  
**Example:** Top 1% writers: Polarize • Network • Edit later • Write first • Are concise • Cut the fluff • Use bullet points • Use white space • Write as they speak • Avoid difficult words • Vary sentence length • Write in an active voice.

## A3. SOCIAL PROOF
**Formula:** "I did [X free/valuable thing]. This allowed me to:" • Collect testimonials • Identify common pain points • Give free value to my audience. [One-line closer.] **Visual:** Testimonials page/screenshot.  
**Example:** I did over 20 free coaching calls. This allowed me to: • Collect testimonials • Identify common pain points • Give free value to my audience. Overdeliver for free.

## A4. CASE STUDY
**Formula:** "[Name] went from [metric A] to [metric B]. After [timeframe] of working together." "Here's the [N] step process we used to [outcome]:" **Visuals:** Before/after profile or metrics screenshots.  
**Example:** Serkan went from 75 to 1200 followers. After 6 weeks of working together. Here's the 7 step process we used to outgrow 99% of creators:

## A5. DEEP DIVES (borrowed authority)
**Formula:** "[Person/product] is overrated / doesn't work — is what haters say. But I [invested/did X] and it [transformed Y]. Here are [N] powerful ideas I learned that you won't find anywhere else." **Visuals:** Course/product graphic, framework diagrams (e.g. 8 Human Desires, Find a Problem loop).  
**Example:** Dan Koe is overrated. His strategies don't work. Is what his jealous haters say. But I took his $150 2-hour writer course and it transformed my writing. Here are 7 powerful ideas I learned that you won't find anywhere else on X.

## A6. NICHE INSIGHTS
**Formula:** "[Common belief] is overrated." "[Metric] [improved] when I [did X] (with the same [thing])." "Focus on [what actually works]." **Visual:** Analytics card (impressions, profile clicks, new followers, conversion rate).  
**Example:** Bios are overrated. My profile conversion doubled when I started writing long-form (with the same bio). Focus on quality content.

## A7. PROOF
**Formula:** "I hit [specific result] in less than [timeframe]." Bullets: [Grind reality]: Zero days off, Countless mistakes, Multiple failed offers. "It was hard but I wouldn't change anything." [Lesson]: "Take the time and effort to [action] and you'll win. [Punchline]." **Visual:** Revenue/dashboard screenshot.  
**Example:** I hit $10,000/mo in less than 10 months. One hell of a grind: Zero days off, Countless mistakes, Multiple failed offers. It was hard but I wouldn't change anything. Take the time and effort to stack skills and you'll win. Competence converts.

## A8. HOW-TO'S / ADVICE
**Formula:** "I [achieved X] in [timeframe]." "Here's what I would do if I had to **start over**:" (+ thread or list).  
**Example:** I became a full-time X creator in 4 months. Here's what I would do if I had to **start over**:

## A9. SHOWING COMPETENCE (borrowed authority × N)
**Formula:** "[Skill] is a superpower." Benefits: More leads, More authority, More followers. [CTA.] **Sub-format:** "I spent [X] hours [consuming authority's content]. Here are the top [N] [habits/ideas/lessons] that will [benefit]." **Visual:** Headshot of authority + framework or list.  
**Example (main):** Writing viral posts is a superpower. More leads. More authority. More followers. Learn to write to print money.  
**Example (embedded):** I spent 100+ hours listening to 100+ Huberman podcasts. Here are the top 5 habits I learned that will change your life.

---

**Quick map for 60 tweets + 1 short growth + 1 authority:**
- **60 tweets:** Use growth templates 1–30 (mix frameworks). Can weave in authority angles (strategies, callouts, lists).
- **1 short growth post:** One template (e.g. 13, 14, 22, or 25) — one tight post.
- **1 medium-form authority:** Use authority types A1–A9. Structure: hook (result/story/contrarian) → 5–7 body tweets (process, strategies, proof, niche insight, or borrowed-authority deep dive) → CTA. Add slides/images for tweets where it fits (process, case study, proof, deep dive).
