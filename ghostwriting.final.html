STEP 1

We run a voice interview

This is where you “write” the content without writing. A short call — like a podcast — so I capture how you think, sell, and talk.

  • Your life & stories
  • Your expertise
  • What you never want said

I record what matters, lock your voice rules, and turn that into a content system for X or LinkedIn.

STEP 2

I create the plan + posts

I map the topics that fit your offer, then write the posts in your voice — not generic AI filler.

  • Clear content pillars
  • Posts that sound like you
  • A rhythm you can keep

You get drafts ready to review. X, LinkedIn, or both — each channel run properly, not diluted.

STEP 3

You approve. I manage the rest.

Nothing goes live without you. After you say yes, I schedule, publish, and keep the channel moving.

  • Approval before publish
  • Scheduling handled
  • Monthly review

You stay in control of the message. I handle the operating work so the account doesn’t go quiet between meetings.

Objections

Straight answers.

Price, voice, timelines, guarantees — the questions founders ask before they DM.

How fast will I see results?
Think in 3 months, not 30 days. Month one is foundation — voice, positioning, content system, first batches shipping. Month two is momentum. Month three is when the channel usually starts pulling its weight. A personal brand doesn’t grow like a switch you flip. If someone promises overnight growth, they’re lying — they’ll take your money and run. I don’t do that. I install the system and we compound.
Why a 3-month package if billing is month-to-month?
No lock-in — you can cancel anytime. I’m just honest about how the work works. The first month is mostly foundation. Pulling out before 90 days is usually right when the system would have started paying off. So I sell it as a 3-month package mindset: stay long enough for the loop to compound.
Will the content actually sound like me?
Every engagement starts with a deep voice setup: your stories, your phrases, your opinions, your no-go zones. Clients regularly tell me even close friends couldn't tell which posts were theirs and which were mine. That's the bar.
Do I have to approve every post before it ships?
You get to. Every piece runs by you before it goes live. Revisions are unlimited. Most clients move to a glance-and-ship routine after a few weeks once the voice is locked — but the choice is always yours.
Ghostwriting or coaching — what’s the difference?
Done-for-you ghostwriting: I write in your voice. You approve. I publish. LinkedIn-first, X optional. Done-with-you coaching: You stay the writer. I audit, position, give weekly direction, and review drafts before they go live. Same standard. Different involvement. DM GHOST or ADVISE and tell me which lane.
Can I do X and LinkedIn together?
Yes — on ghostwriting you can choose LinkedIn, X, or both. We scope each platform properly so it’s not one diluted mix pasted twice. Coaching is usually focused on one primary channel first so the system sticks.
How does pricing work?
Interested in the price? Send me a DM — write GHOST or ADVISE. We talk fit first (offer, channel, goal), then I share numbers. No public price list on purpose: fit first, price second.
Is there a contract or lock-in?
Month-to-month. No lock-in. If it's not working, you walk. Most of the value compounds after the foundation — so I'm direct: pulling out before 90 days is usually when the system would have started paying off.
Can you guarantee the account will grow — or income?
No on specific income. No on guaranteed virality. Anyone who promises that is lying — take the money and run. What I guarantee is the work: voice setup, consistent shipping, strategy, revisions, and communication. Accounts that stay in the system for the 3-month horizon and take the work seriously grow. That's the honest version.
