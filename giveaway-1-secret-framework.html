My Secret Framework

How I structure all my X content so it's not random. One page. No fluff.

Most people post whatever comes to mind and wonder why growth is inconsistent. One day a tweet pops; the next week nothing. The fix isn't more posts—it's a simple structure.

GAP means you rotate between three pillars: Growth (reach + value so new people and the algorithm notice you), Authority (trust + proof so people believe you know your stuff), and Personality (connection + relatability so people like you, not just learn from you). Each pillar does a different job. Use all three and your feed stops feeling random.

Below you get the goal and templates for each pillar, plus real tweet examples you can adapt. At the end there's a quick list of all 12 templates you can use across GAP.

G — Growth

Goal: Follower growth, reach, saves, shares. Clear value and hooks so the algorithm and new people notice you. These tweets are often short (1–4 lines) and easy to consume.

Use list tweets, Stop X Start Y, comparison/opposites, powerful one-liners, hook-list-takeaway, rule of 3, repetition list, and challenging common beliefs. Tie in: high-income skills, goals, opportunity cost, simplicity, freedom, personal responsibility, you are your biggest competitor.

Examples:

You don't need more time. You need fewer distractions. One hour of focused writing beats five hours of scattered "productivity."
Stop waiting for the perfect idea. Start posting the decent one. Done beats perfect every time.
5 things that changed my X growth: 1. Post at the same time daily 2. Reply before I post 3. One thread per week that taught something 4. Stop editing for an hour 5. Ship it.
You are your biggest competitor. Beat yesterday's version.
Replace excuses. Replace "I don't have time" with "I didn't prioritize it." Replace "I'm not ready" with "I'll learn by doing." Language shapes behavior.

A — Authority

Goal: Prove expertise. Your numbers, your method, your results. This is where people decide you know what you're talking about. Authority posts build trust and position you as the person to follow in your niche.

Post before/after results, how you win, niche insights plus metrics, how-to's, proof (followers, revenue). Use 2×3 comparison (before/after), the callout (quote an excuse then counter it), hook-list-takeaway, challenging common beliefs, short personal story (your journey). Topics: I hit X followers, I made my first money, writing, high-income skills, simplicity.

Examples:

How I got my first 1K followers: 1. Same 8 templates every week 2. Reply before I post 3. One thread that actually taught something. No hacks. Just consistency.
"I don't have time to post." Wrong. You have time to scroll. Repurpose 10 minutes into one tweet. Do it for 30 days and see what changes.
Before: Posting whenever, hoping something stuck. After: 30 Growth, 30 Authority, 30 Personality. Same effort. Predictable results. Structure beats randomness.
You don't need a degree to build an audience. You need a high-income skill and the courage to ship. The rest is noise.
Business is like chess. It's about strengthening your position over time, not rushing for the king. Build the audience first; then sell.

P — Personality

Goal: Likeability, relatability. Know, like, trust. People follow accounts they connect with, not just accounts that teach. Personality posts make you human—stories, values, behind-the-scenes, milestones.

Use short personal story, rule of 3, the callout, stop X start Y, repetition list, metaphor, powerful one-liner. Content types: ideas (one big idea + list), stories (past → present, lesson at end), transformations (before/after list), milestones (hit X followers / first $), values (your rules), behind-the-scenes, goals in public, lifestyle. Topics: failure → success, perfectionism, goals, simplicity, freedom, acknowledging weakness.

Examples:

I used to think I had to sound smart. Now I just sound like me. Engagement went up. So did my peace of mind.
2019: Scrolling. Complaining. No audience. 2024: Writing daily. Building in public. Same person. Different habits. You can do the same.
Build in public. Share the process. Let the audience grow with you.
I hit rock bottom before I hit my first 1K followers. The only thing I'd do differently is start sooner. Start writing today.
My favorite thing about being a creator: controlling my time. Making money is nice. But the freedom to choose when and how I work is priceless.

12 templates you can use across GAP

Assign each tweet to one pillar when you write. Mix and match.

  • 1. Comparison / Opposites — X vs Y. "You don't need X. You need Y."
  • 2. Powerful One-Liner — Single punchy line (mindset, truth, reframe)
  • 3. List Tweets — Hook + bullet list (skills, habits, steps)
  • 4. Hook-List-Takeaway — Hook → list → one-line closer
  • 5. Rule of 3 — Three parallel lines (Build / Lift / Write)
  • 6. 2×3 Comparison — Before vs After, or Old you vs New you
  • 7. The Callout — Quote excuse → short counter ("Wrong. You choose…")
  • 8. Repetition List — Same opener repeated (Replace… Replace…)
  • 9. Short Personal Story — Vulnerability or transformation in a few lines
  • 10. Stop X Start Y — Stop [trap]. Start [better behavior].
  • 11. Challenging Common Beliefs — Flip a belief. Replace with your truth.
  • 12. Metaphor (X = Y because Z) — "Business is like chess. It's about…"
