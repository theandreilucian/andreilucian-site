## Template 1: The Personal Story Framework

**Structure:**
```
[Personal Hook/Story Setup]

I [action/situation] and [result/outcome].

Here's what happened:
[Story details - 2-3 lines]

The lesson I learned:
[Key insight]

What this means for you:
[Actionable takeaway]

[Question/CTA]
```

**Example (Lara Acosta Style):**
```
I spent 6 months posting on LinkedIn with no strategy.

Here's what happened:
I posted 5 times per week, but engagement stayed low.

The lesson I learned:
Consistency without strategy = wasted effort.

What this means for you:
Focus on frameworks, not frequency.

What's your biggest challenge with LinkedIn growth?
```

**When to Use:**
- Personal transformation stories
- Lessons learned
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Career/business journey

## Template 2: The Framework Breakdown

**Structure:**
```

Most people [wrong approach].

[Framework Name]:
1. [Step 1 with explanation]
2. [Step 2 with explanation]
3. [Step 3 with explanation]

I've used this to [result].

[Question/CTA]
```

**Example (Lara Acosta Style):**
```
Most people write LinkedIn posts that get ignored.

But here's the framework that works:

THE 3-PART STRUCTURE:
1. Hook that creates curiosity
   - Start with a bold statement
   - Challenge common belief

2. Value in the middle
   - Share a framework
   - Provide actionable insight

3. Engagement at the end
   - Ask a specific question
   - Invite discussion

I've used this to get 10K+ views consistently.

What's your approach to writing engaging posts?
```

**When to Use:**
- Educational content
- How-to guides
- Process breakdowns
- Strategy sharing
