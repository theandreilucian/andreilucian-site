How I grew 3,000 → 4,380

90 days. 3 posts. 50 comments. 20 DMs. That’s it.

The 90 days

All I did:

  • Post 3x/day
  • Comment 50x/day
  • Send 20 DMs/day to people who already talked

I still run a version of this at 5.5K.

From 0

Nobody DMed me back. Nobody liked anything. I tweeted at a wall for months.

I stuck with it. It’s a 4-figure business now. Don’t quit.

Now (5.5K → 10K)

  • 4 posts, spaced — don’t dump them
  • 11:30 is always a list + “What would you add?”
  • Reply to every comment for 60 min
  • 30 real comments on other accounts (mini-posts)
  • No silent days — that’s how impressions dropped 45%

Posts that printed

  • Stop X. Start Y.

  • “Aren’t that smart” + 3 verbs
  • Wall tweets → Don’t quit.
