How I grew 3,000 → 5,600

6 months. Same thing. Every day.

No new personality. No new tactic every Monday. I ran this until the number moved.

Every day

  • Post from the 10 templates below (replace the blue blanks)
  • 30 comments on other accounts (mini-posts, not “great post”)
  • Send 5 DMs to people who already talked
  • Reply to all comments on my posts

The 10 templates

Blue text in brackets = you fill it in. Then hit post.

01 · Stop / Start

Kill a bad habit. Name the replacement.

Stop doing [X]. Start doing [Y].
02 · You don't need / You need

Kill the safe option. Name the real lever.

You don't need [X]. You need [Y]. That's it.
03 · Playbook

3 daily actions + a result with numbers.

My strategy was simple: - [action 1] - [action 2] - [action 3] [timeframe] [start] → [end]
04 · Origin

3 things that went wrong. Then you stayed.

When I first started [doing X] - [failure 1] - [failure 2] - [failure 3] I stuck with it. [result]
05 · List + question

5 bullets. Last line always the same.

[Topic]: • [item 1] • [item 2] • [item 3] • [item 4] • [item 5] What would you add?
06 · Ladder

Show the ugly months, then the win.

Month 1: [number] Month 2: [number] Month 3: [number] Month 13: [number] Imagine if I had quit.
07 · Hammer

One blunt belief. Three verbs. Close it.

Most [people] aren't [belief]. They [verb]. They [verb]. They [verb]. That's it.
08 · Excuse → No

Quote the lie. Kill it in two words. Then the truth.

"[excuse]" No. [the truth]
09 · Receipt

A before → after. Time. What you did.

[start] → [end] [timeframe] [what you did] That's it.
10 · Not X. Y.

They blame the wrong thing. Name the real one.

Your [thing they blame] isn't the problem. Your [real lever] is. [one next step]

That's the whole game

6 months of that = 3,000 → 5,600.

Silent days kill it. New tactics kill it. Collecting templates kills it.

Fill. Post. Comment 30. DM 5. Reply to everyone. Repeat.

Don't quit 10% in. The graph moves after the work — not during the refresh.
