60 Instagram Posts for Growth

30 Carousels + 30 Reels | Based on analysis of Dan Koe, Lara Acosta, Sijin Noorman, Dakota Robertson, and Justin Welsh

How to Use This Content

• Carousels: Create 5-10 slides per carousel. Use Canva or similar tools to design visually appealing slides.

• Reels: Use these scripts as talking points. Film yourself speaking or use text overlays with trending audio.

• Posting Schedule: Post 1 carousel and 1 reel per day for 30 days, or mix them throughout the week.

• Customization: Add your specific numbers, stories, and experiences to make each post unique.

• Hashtags: Use 5-10 relevant hashtags per post. Mix popular and niche hashtags.
