## GIVEAWAY HOOK OPTIONS

### Hook 1: Results-Focused (RECOMMENDED)
**"I helped a client go from 0 to 800 followers in 24 hours. Here's the exact framework I used—and the swipe file with 50+ top-performing tweets that you can copy and adapt."**

**Why it works:**
- Specific result (800 followers)
- Time-bound (24 hours)
- Clear value (framework + swipe file)
- Actionable (copy and adapt)

### Hook 2: Problem-Solution
**"Struggling to grow on X? I've helped 25+ clients go from 0 to 1000 followers in 30 days. Here's my complete framework + 50+ proven tweet templates—free."**

**Why it works:**
- Addresses pain point
- Social proof (25+ clients)
- Clear outcome
- Emphasizes "free"

### Hook 3: Framework-Focused
**"The exact framework I used to help a client gain 800 followers in one day. Includes 50+ proven tweet templates, engagement strategies, and a 30-day action plan."**

**Why it works:**
- Emphasizes framework
- Specific result
- Lists deliverables
- Action-oriented

### Hook 4: Quick Win
**"Want to go from 0 to 1000 followers in 30 days? I'm giving away my complete X growth framework + 50+ top-performing tweet templates. Everything you need to start growing today."**

**Why it works:**
- Question hook (engages)
- Clear timeline
- Emphasizes "giving away"
- Immediate action

### Hook 5: Story-Driven
**"Last month, I helped a client with zero followers gain 800 in 24 hours. I'm sharing the exact framework, plus a swipe file with 50+ proven tweets you can use today."**

**Why it works:**
- Story format
- Specific timeframe
- Clear value proposition
- Immediate usability

**RECOMMENDED:** Hook 1 (Most specific, most compelling)

## BEST PRACTICES FOR GIVEAWAY POSTS

1. **Post at Peak Times:** 9 AM, 12 PM, 5 PM EST
2. **Pin to Profile:** Pin your best giveaway post
3. **Post Multiple Times:** Share 3-5 times over the week
4. **Engage with Replies:** Reply to everyone who comments
5. **Track Results:** Monitor which hook performs best
6. **Follow Up:** Send the guide promptly to those who reply
