EMAIL 2 — WRITING
Subject: Your first line is everything

Hey,

The first line of your post decides everything.

If the first line is boring, nobody reads the rest. It does not matter how good your idea is.

I spent months writing posts that got 50 views. Then I changed one thing: I stopped trying to sound smart. I started trying to sound human.

Bad hook: "Here are 5 tips for better content."
Good hook: "I spent 6 months writing posts nobody read. Then I found one shift that changed everything."

See the difference?

One sounds like a textbook. One sounds like a real person.

Good writing is not about big words. It is about making people stop scrolling and think: I need to read this.

The best posts I write follow a simple path:

1. Hook — grab attention
2. Story — make it real
3. Value — teach something useful

That is it. No fancy tricks. Just clear words that help someone.
