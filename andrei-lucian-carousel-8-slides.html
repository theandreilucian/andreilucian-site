10 things I had to unlearn to grow on LinkedIn

More posts = more reach.

(Nope. Better posts do.)

I need to sound professional.

(I need to sound like me.)

Nobody cares what I think.

(They care if it helps.)

I have to post every day.

(I have to show up consistently.)

The algorithm is the enemy.

(The algorithm rewards value.)

I need a big following first.

(I need one person who gets it.)
