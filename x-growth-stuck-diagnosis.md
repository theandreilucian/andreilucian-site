**Issue:** 100 comments per day = spreading yourself too thin

**Why This Hurts:**
- Low-value, generic comments don't build relationships
- You're engaging with everyone instead of the RIGHT people
- No strategic targeting of accounts that can actually help you grow
- Quantity engagement ≠ meaningful engagement

**The Fix:**
- Reduce to 30-50 HIGH-QUALITY engagements
- Focus on Tier 1 (top players) and Tier 2 (growing accounts)
- Engage with accounts in YOUR niche
- Make every comment valuable, not just present

**Issue:** Engaging with random accounts instead of strategic targets

**Issue:** 3 posts daily is good, but are they HIGH-IMPACT?

**Questions to Ask:**
- Are your hooks strong enough?
- Do you provide actionable value every time?
- Are you using proven formulas?
- Is your content differentiated?
- Do you have a clear CTA?

**The Fix:**

- Use proven thread formulas
- Spend 50% of time on the hook
- Ensure every post provides value

### Problem #4: Profile Optimization
**Issue:** People might engage but don't follow

**Check:**

**The Fix:**

- Include social proof

### Problem #5: No Conversion Strategy
**Issue:** Engagement doesn't convert to followers

**Why:**

- Content is good but not follow-worthy
- Missing CTAs
- Not building authority

**The Fix:**

- Create follow-worthy content series
