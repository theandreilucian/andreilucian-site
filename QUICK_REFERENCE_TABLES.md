## 💡 Key Principles (Focused Approach)

1. **Focus on 2:** X and LinkedIn are your main platforms (90% of time)
2. **Content Only on Secondary:** Threads, Substack, Instagram = POST ONLY, NO engagement (10% of time)
3. **No Guilt:** It's okay to ignore engagement on secondary platforms
4. **Quality > Quantity:** Better to excel on 2 than be mediocre on 5
5. **Engagement Only on Primary:** ONLY engage on X and LinkedIn (comments, DMs, likes)
6. **GAP Framework:** Apply to X and LinkedIn only
7. **Test & Iterate:** Focus optimization on X and LinkedIn
