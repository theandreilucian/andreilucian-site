Introduction

Two DOE flagships: Discovery at Oak Ridge (OLCF) — follow-on to Frontier, delivery 2028 — and El Capitan at Lawrence Livermore (LLNL) — Cray EX255a with MI300A APUs, in production at >1.8 EFLOPS HPL. One future, one current; both define the public-sector exascale stack.

Discovery (OLCF)

Jan 30, 2026 · #system, #usa/doe/ascr.

Follow-on to Frontier at Oak Ridge. Delivery 2028. (From the press release.)

[Image: Discovery (OLCF)]
System, cabinet, or diagram

Platform: Cray GX. CPU: Venice. GPU: AMD MI430X. Interconnect: next-generation Slingshot. Storage: Cray E2000 Lustre appliances; Cray K3000 DAOS appliances. ASCR path: next-gen Cray, Venice + MI430X, Lustre + DAOS; 2028.

El Capitan (LLNL)

Dec 05, 2025 · #usa/doe/nnsa, #system.

Cray EX255a / MI300A at Livermore. In production, >1.8 EFLOPS HPL.

[Image: El Capitan (LLNL)]
System, cabinet, or node

HPL: max 1.809 EFLOPS (Nov 2025); debut 1.742 EFLOPS (Nov 2024). Scale: 11,264 EX255a nodes, 45,056 MI300A APUs, 88 cabinets, 704 Rabbit blades (8/cabinet). 34.8 MW peak, 30 MW HPL. Network: Slingshot-11, dragonfly. Node: 4× MI300A, 512 GB HBM3 (8-high), fully interconnected. Storage: 8 Rabbit blades/rack on Slingshot; global Lustre Merced. NNSA flagship; APU-based exascale reference.

Why these systems matter

Discovery — OLCF next: Cray GX, Venice, MI430X, next-gen Slingshot, E2000 + K3000. 2028, ASCR. El Capitan — LLNL now: EX255a, 45k MI300A, 1.809 EF HPL, Slingshot-11, Rabbit + Merced. NNSA, APU deployment model.
