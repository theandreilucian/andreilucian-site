⏰ TIMEZONE CONVERSION GUIDE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

If you're in a different timezone, adjust these times:

EST/EDT (Eastern): Use times as listed
PST/PDT (Pacific): Subtract 3 hours
CST/CDT (Central): Subtract 1 hour
MST/MDT (Mountain): Subtract 2 hours
GMT (UK): Add 5 hours (EST) or 4 hours (EDT)
CET (Central Europe): Add 6 hours (EST) or 5 hours (EDT)

EXAMPLE: If you're in PST and want to post at 8 AM EST:
8:00 AM EST = 5:00 AM PST
