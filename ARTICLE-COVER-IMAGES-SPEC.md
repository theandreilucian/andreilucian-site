# Article Cover Images Specification

## Overview
Both articles need black & white engraving-style cover images, similar to Dan Koe's "How to fix your entire life in 1 day" video thumbnail.

## Image Style Requirements
- **Style:** Black and white, detailed engraving/etching style
- **Composition:** Dramatic, high contrast
- **Texture:** Swirling, detailed textures throughout
- **Mood:** Intense, transformative, powerful
- **Format:** High resolution, suitable for video thumbnails and article headers

---

## Article 1: "How to Rebuild Yourself From Zero in 7 Days"

### Image Description:
**Person climbing out of a deep pit/abyss, reaching upward toward light**

- **Main Subject:** A person (gender-neutral silhouette) positioned in the lower portion of the frame
- **Action:** Climbing upward, reaching toward light at the top
- **Setting:** Deep pit or abyss with dark, swirling textures
- **Light Source:** Bright light streaming from above, creating dramatic contrast
- **Details:**
  - Person's hands reaching upward, fingers straining toward the light
  - Body positioned diagonally, showing effort and movement
  - Swirling textures around the pit walls suggesting depth and struggle
  - Light rays breaking through darkness at the top
  - Engraving-style crosshatching and detailed line work throughout
- **Emotional Tone:** Hope, struggle, transformation, upward movement

---

## Article 2: "How to Transform Your Identity in 24 Hours"

### Image Description:
**Person breaking through a wall/mask, fragments falling away, revealing their true self underneath**

- **Main Subject:** A person (gender-neutral silhouette) in the center of the frame
- **Action:** Breaking through or emerging from a wall/mask
- **Setting:** Fragmented wall or mask pieces falling away
- **Details:**
  - Person's face/upper body emerging from broken fragments
  - Pieces of the old identity (wall/mask fragments) falling away
  - Swirling textures in the background suggesting transformation
  - Light breaking through from behind the person
  - Engraving-style detailed line work showing cracks and fragments
  - Sense of liberation and emergence
- **Emotional Tone:** Liberation, transformation, authenticity, breaking free

## Technical Specifications

### Dimensions
- **Recommended:** 1920x1080 (16:9 aspect ratio) for video thumbnails
- **Alternative:** 1200x675 for web articles
- **Minimum:** 800x450

### Color
- **Mode:** Grayscale (black and white only)
- **Contrast:** High contrast for dramatic effect
- **No color:** Pure black and white, no grays (or minimal grays for texture)

### File Format
- **Primary:** PNG (for transparency if needed)
- **Alternative:** JPG (for smaller file size)
- **Quality:** High resolution, suitable for print if needed

### Style Reference
- Similar to Dan Koe's video thumbnail style
- Engraving/etching aesthetic
- Detailed, intricate line work
- Dramatic composition
- High contrast lighting

---

## Notes for Designer

1. Both images should feel cohesive and part of the same series
2. Maintain the same artistic style (engraving/etching) across both
3. Focus on dramatic, transformative moments
4. Use light and shadow to create depth and emotion
5. Ensure the images work well at thumbnail size (readable and impactful)
6. The person should be gender-neutral or ambiguous
7. Focus on the emotional journey, not specific features
