AMD MI325X — CDNA 3, memory bump

Oct 10, 2024 · #GPU · Q4 2024. MI325X is the memory bump to MI300X. Same CDNA 3 compute, upgraded memory and power. Positioned for high-memory inference and training.

[Image: AMD MI325X]
Product or block diagram

Per GPU: Same compute as MI300X (8 XCDs, 304 CUs, 1,216 matrix cores, 2.1 GHz, 2:4 sparsity). 256 GB HBM3E, 6 TB/s. 7× Infinity Fabric D2D, 1×16 PCIe Gen5. 1000 W max TBP.

Launch: 1.3 PF FP16, 2.6 PF FP8 (same as MI300X). Gain is capacity and bandwidth for larger models and longer context.

AMD MI355X — CDNA 4, inference-optimized

Jun 19, 2025 · #GPU. First CDNA 4 GPU. Optimized for inferencing: lower FP64/FP32 than MI300X, higher 16-bit and 8-bit; adds FP6 and FP4. Low-precision strong for inference; FP64 high. 8-way OAM UBB.

[Image: AMD MI355X]
Product or block diagram

Per GPU: 8 XCDs, 256 CUs (32 per XCD), 16,384 stream processors, 1,024 matrix cores; 2.4 GHz; 2:4 sparsity; 288 GB HBM3e, 8 TB/s; 7× 153.6 GB/s Infinity Fabric D2D, 1×16 PCIe Gen5; 1400 W max.

AMD MI430X — Next-gen CDNA, HBM4, HPC

Jan 08, 2026 · #GPU. Future AMD GPU on next-generation CDNA, aimed at HPC with FP64 hardware support.

[Image: AMD MI430X]
Product or block diagram

Headline specs:

  • 432 GB HBM4
  • 19.6 TB/s memory bandwidth

Major step up for very large models, long context, and memory-bound HPC. FP64 focus = scientific simulation and double-precision. Full XCD/CU/TFLOPS details as AMD discloses.

Why these three matter

MI325X — Same CDNA 3 compute, 256 GB HBM3E, 6 TB/s. More memory and bandwidth per GPU; high-memory inference and training.

MI355X — CDNA 4, inference-first. FP6/FP4, 288 GB HBM3e, 8 TB/s, 1400 W. Inference-first; stronger FP64 than typical inference parts.

MI430X — Next-gen CDNA, 432 GB HBM4, 19.6 TB/s. Placeholder for the next jump in capacity, bandwidth, and HPC.

With Article 1, this completes the arc from CDNA 2 through CDNA 4 and into the next node.
