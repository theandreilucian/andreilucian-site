# Kade's Profile Picture Specifications

## DESIRED STYLE

**Aesthetic:** Black and white, minimalist, stoic, professional
**Vibe:** "No fluff. Only execution." - Clean, sharp, masculine, disciplined

---

## TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

### Image Requirements:
- **Format:** Square (1:1 ratio) - minimum 400x400 pixels, recommended 1000x1000 pixels
- **File format:** PNG or JPG
- **Color mode:** Grayscale (black and white)
- **File size:** Under 2MB (optimized for web)

### Background:
- **Color:** Light gray (#E5E5E5 to #F5F5F5) or pure white (#FFFFFF)
- **Style:** Solid, plain, uncluttered
- **No patterns, textures, or gradients**
- Clean, minimalist backdrop

### Subject (Person):
- **Color treatment:** Black and dark grays only
- **Contrast:** High contrast between subject and background
- **Tone:** Dark, serious, stoic
- **Expression:** Neutral, focused, confident, no smile
- **Lighting:** Soft, even lighting with subtle shadows for definition

---

## EDITING INSTRUCTIONS

### Using Photoshop:
1. **Open the image**
2. **Convert to Grayscale:**
   - Image → Mode → Grayscale
   - Or: Image → Adjustments → Black & White

3. **Adjust Background:**
   - Select background (Magic Wand or Select Subject → Inverse)
   - Fill with light gray (#E5E5E5) or white (#FFFFFF)
   - Ensure clean, solid background

4. **Adjust Subject Contrast:**
   - Image → Adjustments → Levels
   - Darken the subject (person) to black/dark gray
   - Increase contrast for definition
   - Target: Person should be in black/dark tones

5. **Fine-tune:**
   - Image → Adjustments → Curves (if needed)
   - Darken shadows, maintain highlights
   - Ensure face is clearly visible but in dark tones

6. **Final adjustments:**
   - Sharpen slightly (Filter → Sharpen → Unsharp Mask)
   - Ensure clean edges
   - Save as PNG or high-quality JPG

### Using Canva:
1. **Upload image**
2. **Apply filter:** "Grayscale" or "Black & White"
3. **Adjust background:**
   - Use background remover tool
   - Add solid gray or white background
4. **Adjust contrast:**
   - Increase contrast slider
   - Darken shadows
   - Lighten background if needed
5. **Export:** PNG or JPG, 1000x1000 pixels

### Using Online Tools (Photopea, Remove.bg, etc.):
1. **Remove background** (if needed)
2. **Convert to grayscale**
3. **Add solid gray/white background**
4. **Adjust contrast** to darken subject
5. **Export** in square format
