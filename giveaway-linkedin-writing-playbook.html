The LinkedIn Writing Guide

Profile → templates → networking → offer. No fluff.

LinkedIn is the best platform for building authority, trust, and inbound opportunities — if you use it with consistency.

This guide pulls the highest-leverage parts from my full playbook. Steal the structure. Rewrite in your voice. Ship.

Part 1 — Profile (fix this first)

Your profile should answer 3 questions in 5 seconds:

  1. Who you help
  2. What result you help them get
  3. Why they should trust you

Focus on headline, banner, profile photo, and about section. Keep your message specific and proof-based.

Bad: "Marketing Professional | Passionate About Growth"
Good: "I help creators turn daily writing into inbound clients | 16K+ on LinkedIn"

Part 2 — 6 post templates

Template 1 — Simplify

Structure

People overcomplicate {thing} → Avoid list → Instead list → Focus for {timeframe}

People overcomplicate LinkedIn growth. Listen, if you're a beginner creator: Avoid: • Posting without a profile that converts • Chasing viral hooks with zero proof • Connecting cold with no context • Quitting after 2 weeks of silence Instead: • Fix headline + about first • Post 3x/week with one template • Comment on 10 posts before you publish Focus on this for the first 30 days. Build from there.

Template 2 — Wrong vs Right

Structure

How you think you should {action} → How you actually {action} → Strong brands beat strong CTAs

How you think you should grow on LinkedIn: • Post once a month when you "feel inspired" • Copy what big accounts do • Connect with 50 strangers per day How you actually grow on LinkedIn: • Educational content around writing and personal branding • Free resources that solve real problems • Consistent value and proof from your own results Strong CTAs are good. Strong brands are better.

Template 3 — Transformation

Structure

{Timeframe} ago I stopped {action} → Misconceptions → Achievements → Closer

6 months ago, I stopped treating LinkedIn like a resume dump. And no: • It didn't kill my reach • It didn't make me look "unprofessional" • It didn't require 2 hours per post Since I stopped posting random updates: • Inbound DMs went up • Comments got longer • I stopped rewriting from zero every day Turns out consistency beats perfection.

Template 4 — Struggles

Structure

Too many success stories → Not enough struggles → Your 3 fails → Reality check

There's too many "I hit 10K followers on LinkedIn." But not enough "I posted into the void for 4 months." This year I've: • Published posts that got 3 likes • Rewrote the same hook 6 times • Almost quit twice That's the reality of building in public.

Template 5 — 60 Second Masterclass

Structure

Title + credibility → 7 actionable tips → Take reader A → B

60-second LinkedIn Writing Masterclass (From someone who's posted daily for 2+ years) 1. Cut the fluff — one idea per post 2. Hook in the first line, not the third 3. Use white space — short paragraphs win 4. Add a photo when you can 5. Reply to every comment in the first hour 6. End with a question or clear CTA 7. Repurpose your best X posts

Template 6 — Challenging Beliefs

Structure

Challenge common belief → 3 contrasting ideas → Action steps → Timeframe

"You need to post every day on LinkedIn." Wrong. You need to post consistently with a system. You need to reply to comments like they matter. You need to show up even when the post flops. Fix your profile. Use templates. Comment before you publish. Do it for 90 days. Watch what changes.

Part 3 — Networking loop

  1. Build a target list — 5–10 people in your niche
  2. Comment meaningfully — add insight, not "Great post!"
  3. Connect after real interaction — never cold pitch first
  4. Reply to every quality comment — first hour matters most

No hacks. Just reps. Trust compounds.

Part 4 — 7-day action checklist

  • Day 1: Fix headline + about section
  • Day 2: Pick 2 templates from this guide
  • Day 3: Write + publish post #1 (add a photo)
  • Day 4: Comment on 10 posts in your niche
  • Day 5: Write + publish post #2
  • Day 6: Build your target list (5–10 names)
  • Day 7: Reply to every comment + comment on 5 target accounts

Repeat. Don't quit 10% into success.
