Instagram Growth Tips

Posting Strategy

  • Post 5-7x per week minimum
  • Best times: 7-9 AM, 12-1 PM, 6-8 PM
  • Use Reels for maximum reach
  • Mix Reels, carousels, and single posts

Engagement

  • Reply to ALL comments within 2 hours
  • Engage with 10-20 accounts daily
  • Use Stories 2-3x per day
  • Ask questions in captions

Hashtags

  • Use 20-30 hashtags per post
  • Mix broad, medium, and niche tags
  • Change hashtags each post
  • Research competitor hashtags

Content Mix

  • 80% value, 20% promotional
  • Show transformations
  • Share behind-the-scenes
  • Provide actionable tips
