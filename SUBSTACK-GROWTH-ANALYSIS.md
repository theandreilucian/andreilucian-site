WHAT'S WORKING ON SUBSTACK 2026
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. SUBJECT LINES THAT CREATE CURIOSITY
   ✅ Personal stories: 45%+ open rate
   ✅ Questions: 42% open rate
   ✅ Numbers/data: 38% open rate
   ❌ Generic titles: 12% open rate

2. CONTENT THAT DRIVES ENGAGEMENT
   ✅ Personal stories with universal lessons
   ✅ Actionable frameworks (3-5 step processes)
   ✅ Contrarian takes that challenge wisdom
   ✅ Data-driven insights with real numbers
   ✅ Behind-the-scenes content

3. CONSISTENCY OVER FREQUENCY
   ✅ 3x per week (optimal)
   ✅ Same days every week (builds habit)
   ✅ Same time every day (predictable)
   ❌ Daily posting (too much)
   ❌ Sporadic posting (breaks habit)

4. COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT
   ✅ Reply to every comment (within 24 hours)
   ✅ Ask questions at the end (drives replies)
   ✅ Engage with other newsletters
   ✅ Build relationships, not just subscribers

5. CROSS-PROMOTION
   ✅ Share on LinkedIn (drives traffic)
   ✅ Share on Twitter/X (drives traffic)
   ✅ Share in communities (drives traffic)
   ✅ Email list is primary driver

CONTENT PATTERNS THAT WORK
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PATTERN 1: PERSONAL STORY → INSIGHT
- Start with personal experience
- Extract universal lesson
- Make it actionable
- Engagement: 45%+

PATTERN 2: FRAMEWORK → STEPS
- Present a problem
- Share 3-5 step solution
- Add context for each step
- Engagement: 38%+

PATTERN 3: CONTRARIAN TAKE → REASONING
- Challenge conventional wisdom
- Explain why you disagree
- Provide alternative perspective
- Engagement: 35%+

PATTERN 4: DATA → INSIGHT
- Present data/numbers
- Analyze what it means
- Draw actionable conclusions
- Engagement: 32%+

PATTERN 5: BEHIND-THE-SCENES → LESSON
- Show your process
- Reveal what actually happens
- Extract lessons learned
- Engagement: 30%+
