## 🎯 STRATEGIC ENGAGEMENT BY TIER

### Tier 1: Top Players (10-15 accounts)

**Engagement Strategy:**
- **Frequency:** 2-3 engagements per week per account
- **Quality:** High-value, strategic replies only
- **Approach:** Build relationships, not just comment
- **Time Investment:** 5-10 minutes per engagement

**Comment Strategy:**
```
"[Their insight] is spot on.

I've applied this with 20+ clients and found [specific insight].

The game-changer is [actionable takeaway].

Would love to hear your thoughts on [thoughtful question]."
```

**What to Do:**
- Add value in every reply
- Show your expertise
- Don't self-promote
- Build relationship over time

**What NOT to Do:**
- Generic comments
- Asking for follows
- Copy-paste replies
- Low-value engagement

**Total:** 20-45 Tier 1 engagements per week

---

### Tier 2: Growing Accounts (30-40 accounts)

**Engagement Strategy:**
- **Frequency:** 3-5 engagements per week per account
- **Quality:** Value-driven, peer-level engagement
- **Approach:** Build relationships, cross-promote
- **Time Investment:** 2-5 minutes per engagement

**Comment Strategy:**
```
"This resonates with what I'm seeing.

When I help clients with [specific thing], we focus on [insight].

Here's what's been working: [actionable takeaway].

Would love to hear what's working for you."
```

**What to Do:**
- Share peer insights
- Build mutual relationships
- Support their growth
- Cross-promote opportunities

**What NOT to Do:**
- Generic comments
- One-way engagement
- Ignore their content
- Transactional approach

**Total:** 90-200 Tier 2 engagements per week

---

### Tier 3: Emerging Accounts (50-60 accounts)

**Engagement Strategy:**
- **Frequency:** 1-2 engagements per week per account
- **Quality:** Supportive, value-add engagement
- **Approach:** Build community, mentorship
- **Time Investment:** 1-3 minutes per engagement

**Comment Strategy:**
```
"Keep going with this.

Here's what helped me: [insight].

Specifically, [actionable tip].

This is a journey. You're doing great."
```

**What to Do:**
- Support their journey
- Provide value
- Build community
- Encourage growth

**What NOT to Do:**
- Generic encouragement
- Ignore completely
- Over-engage
- Patronizing tone

**Total:** 50-120 Tier 3 engagements per week

## 🔥 UPGRADE YOUR ENGAGEMENT QUALITY

### Stop Generic Comments

**❌ DON'T:**
```
"Great post!"
"Love this!"
"Thanks for sharing!"
"100%!"
"Totally agree!"
```

**Why These Don't Work:**
- No value added
- Doesn't show expertise
- Won't build relationships
- Wastes time
- Gets ignored

---

### Start Strategic Comments

**✅ DO:**

**Template 1: Value-Add Reply**
```
"[Their insight] is spot on.

I've seen this with 20+ clients I've worked with.

The key difference is [your specific insight].

Most creators miss this because [reason].

Have you found [specific thing] to be true?"
```

**Template 2: Peer Insight**
```
"This resonates with what I'm seeing.

When I help clients with [specific thing], we focus on [insight].

Here's what's been working: [actionable takeaway].

Would love to hear what's working for you."
```

**Template 3: Strategic Question**
```
"[Their insight] is exactly right.

How do you handle [specific challenge]?

I'm working with a client on this right now, and we're finding [insight].

Would love to hear your approach."
```

**Template 4: Relationship Building**
```
"Been following your work on [topic].

This [insight] aligns with what I've learned helping [who you help].

Specifically, [your perspective/example].

Would love to connect and learn more about [specific thing]."
```
