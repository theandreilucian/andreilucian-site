# Daily Action Checklist - FOCUSED APPROACH

## Quick Daily Checklist (~1.5 hours total)

**PRIMARY FOCUS:** X (Twitter) & LinkedIn (90% of time)  
**SECONDARY:** Threads, Substack, Instagram (10% of time - repurpose only)

---

### 🌅 MORNING SESSION (30 min)

#### X (Twitter) - PRIMARY - 15 min
- [ ] Post 1 tweet (GAP framework)
- [ ] 30 comments on other tweets
- [ ] Reply to comments on your tweets

#### LinkedIn - PRIMARY - 15 min
- [ ] Post 1 post (if scheduled - 3-5 posts/week total)
- [ ] 20 comments on other posts
- [ ] Engage in 1-2 groups

---

### 🌆 MIDDAY SESSION (35 min)

#### X (Twitter) - PRIMARY - 15 min
- [ ] Post 1 tweet (GAP framework)
- [ ] 30 comments on other tweets
- [ ] 10 DMs
- [ ] Reply to comments on your tweets

#### LinkedIn - PRIMARY - 15 min
- [ ] 20 comments on other posts
- [ ] 5-10 DMs
- [ ] Engage in 1-2 groups

#### Threads - CONTENT ONLY - 2 min
- [ ] Post 1 post (copy best X tweet from today)
- [ ] ❌ NO engagement (no comments, no DMs, no likes)

#### Substack - CONTENT ONLY - 3 min
- [ ] Post 1 post (repurpose best X/LinkedIn content)
- [ ] ❌ NO engagement (no comments, no DMs, no likes)

---

### 🌙 EVENING SESSION (25 min)

#### X (Twitter) - PRIMARY - 15 min
- [ ] Post 1 tweet (GAP framework)
- [ ] 40 comments on other tweets
- [ ] 10 DMs
- [ ] Reply to ALL comments on your tweets

#### LinkedIn - PRIMARY - 10 min
- [ ] 10 comments on other posts
- [ ] 5-10 DMs
- [ ] Reply to ALL comments on your posts

---

### 📸 INSTAGRAM - CONTENT ONLY (Every 2-3 days, 2 min)
- [ ] Screenshot 1 best X tweet or LinkedIn post
- [ ] Post with simple caption
- [ ] ❌ NO engagement (no comments, no DMs, no likes)

**Total Daily Time:** ~90 minutes (90% on X & LinkedIn)
