📝 Content Templates

1. Comparison / Opposites

Structure: "You don't need X. You need Y."

You don't need a degree.

You need a high-income skill.

You don't need university.

You need YouTube.

Structure:
You don't need [common belief].
You need [better alternative].

2. Powerful One-Liner

Structure: Bold statement with impact

You feel bad because your subconscious mind knows your unrealized potential.

There's not a single problem that's as bad as your mind makes you believe it is.

Structure:
[Bold statement] because [insight].
Use bold for key words.

3. List Tweets

Structure: Topic + bulleted list

The best skills to learn in 2024:

• Sales

• Design

• Writing

• Speaking

• Marketing

Structure:
[Topic/Question]:
• Item 1
• Item 2
• Item 3

4. Hook-List-Takeaway

Structure: Hook + List + Closing statement

How to win in 6 months:

• Learn a skill

• Share advice

• Build an audience

• Do free work for social proof

• Leverage testimonials for paid work

Don't overcomplicate it.

Structure:
[Hook/Question]:
• Step 1
• Step 2
[Closing statement]

5. The Rule of Three

Structure: Three related statements

Build to improve your life.

Lift to improve your body.

Write to improve your mind.

Use mornings to create.

Use afternoons to learn.

Use evenings to reflect.

Structure:
[Action 1] to [outcome 1].
[Action 2] to [outcome 2].
[Action 3] to [outcome 3].

6. 2X3 Comparison

Structure: Before/After with 3 items each

July:

• Restart

• No clarity

• No writing skills

October:

• 1900+ followers

• 20+ coaching calls

• Wrote five e-books

Things move fast when you move fast.

Structure:
[Time 1]:
• State 1
• State 2
[Time 2]:
• Achievement 1
• Achievement 2
[Closing insight]

7. The Callout

Structure: Challenge common excuse/belief

"I don't have discipline."

Wrong.

You choose not to be disciplined.

"I'm a perfectionist."

No.

You're a procrastinator.

Structure:
"[Common excuse]"
[Direct response]
[Truth statement]

8. Repetition List

Structure: Repeat word/phrase + conclusion

Study sales

Study design

Study writing

Study marketing

Study copywriting

Study everything that matters.

Show up

Show up

Show up

Put in the reps.

Structure:
[Action/Word]
[Action/Word] (repeat 3-8x)
[Closing statement]

9. Short Personal Story

Structure: Personal context + transformation

I'm not English.

• I write in English

• I teach in English

• I record in English

And I screw up my English with all of them.

But I couldn't care less.

Stop letting your weaknesses hold you back.

Structure:
[Personal statement]
• Context 1
• Context 2
[Vulnerability]
[Empowerment message]

10. Stop X Start X

Structure: Stop doing X, start doing Y

Stop talking about your goals.

Start executing your content system.

You're collecting dopamine you haven't earned yet.

Earn it through consistency.

Structure:
Stop [bad habit/action].
Start [good habit/action].
[Insight/explanation]

11. Challenging Common Beliefs

Structure: Challenge popular misconception

A degree doesn't mean you're a great thinker.

A degree means you're a great memorizer.

You don't need a degree if you can write well.

Structure:
[Common belief] doesn't mean [assumption].
[Common belief] means [reality].
OR: You don't need [X] if you can [Y].

12. Transformation Story

Structure: Past vs Present transformation

I'm an addict.

2019:

• Weed

• Games

• Nicotine

2023:

• Fitness

• Writing

• Business

Find better addictions.

Structure:
[Opening statement]
[Past time]:
• Old habit 1
[Present time]:
• New habit 1
[Closing insight]
