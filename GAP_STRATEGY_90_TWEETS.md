# GAP Framework Strategy — 90 Tweets

**Your split:** 30 Growth | 30 Personality | 30 Authority

---

## 📐 ALL TEMPLATES (12)

Use these across all three pillars. Assign each draft to one pillar (Growth / Personality / Authority) when you write.

| # | Template | Structure | Best for pillar |
|---|----------|-----------|------------------|
| 1 | **Comparison / Opposites** | X vs Y. "You don't need X. You need Y." | Growth, Authority |
| 2 | **Powerful One-Liner** | Single punchy line (mindset, truth, reframe) | Personality, Growth |
| 3 | **List Tweets** | Hook + bullet list (skills, habits, steps) | Growth, Personality |
| 4 | **Hook-List-Takeaway** | Hook → list → one-line closer ("Don't overcomplicate it.") | Growth, Authority |
| 5 | **Rule of 3** | Three parallel lines (Build/Lift/Write; Past/Future/Present) | Personality, Growth |
| 6 | **2×3 Comparison** | Two columns: Before vs After, or Old you vs New you | Authority, Personality |
| 7 | **The Callout** | Quote excuse → short counter ("Wrong. You choose not to be disciplined.") | Authority, Personality |
| 8 | **Repetition List** | Same opener repeated (Study… Study… / Replace… Replace…) | Growth, Personality |
| 9 | **Short Personal Story** | "I'm not English" / "I'm an addict" — vulnerability or transformation | Personality, Authority |
| 10 | **Stop X Start Y** | Stop [trap]. Start [better behavior]. | Growth, Personality |
| 11 | **Challenging Common Beliefs** | "You don't need a degree. You need a high-income skill." | Authority, Growth |
| 12 | **Metaphor (X = Y because Z)** | "Business is like chess. It's about strengthening position, not rushing." | Personality, Authority |

## 2️⃣ 30 TWEETS — PERSONALITY (Long-form: motivation, discipline, health, learning)

**Goal:** Likeability, relatability, mindset. Motivation, discipline, health, learning.

---

### Best Personality Content (types to use)

Use these **content types** for your 30 Personality tweets (ideas, stories, values, lifestyle, etc.):

| Type | What it is | Example angle |
|------|------------|----------------|
| **Ideas** | Frameworks, reframes, one big idea + short list | "Creators are knowledge compressors. Learn → Take notes → Compress it. Help humans evolve faster." / "You must become a Self-Scientist. Make a plan, Test it, Reflect. Treat your life like a scientific study." |
| **Stories** | Past → present narrative, lesson at the end | "I hit rock bottom. [20 yo: Addicted, Depressed, College dropout.] Today: Full-time creator, Happy and healthy, Time freedom. Here are 5 things you can learn from my story." |
| **Transformations** | Before/after in list form, punchy closer | "I'm an addict. 2019: Weed, Games, Nicotine. 2023: Fitness, Writing, Business. Find better addictions." |
| **Milestones** | Hit X followers / first $ / goal + reflection | "I hit [X] followers. Writing online changed my life. The only thing I'd do differently is start sooner. Start writing today." |
| **Values** | Your rules, principles, non-negotiables | "8 Personal Business Rules: Write daily, Do less better, Prioritize purpose, Never chase clients, Competence is king, Play long-term games, Product over sales tactics, Ignore 99% of noise." |
| **Behind the scenes** | What you're building, setup, process + photo | "Big changes soon: New course, New workshop, New [X]. Let's crush it." + photo (setup, desk, gear) |
| **Goals / building in public** | Public goal + progress + "I'll share lessons here" | "Restarting [X] again soon. Aiming for [goal] by [time]. [X] left. Will share progress and lessons on here!" + workspace/screenshot |
| **Lifestyle** | What you love about the creator life (time, freedom) | "My favorite thing about being a creator: Controlling my time. Making money is nice. But the **freedom** to control your time is priceless." + lifestyle image (e.g. coffee, notebook, view) |

*Tip: Use slides or photos for image tweets (BTS, goals, lifestyle) when it fits.*

## 3️⃣ 30 TWEETS — AUTHORITY (X/Twitter, writing, your results)

**Goal:** Prove expertise. X growth, writing, your numbers and outcomes.

---

### Best Authority Content (types to use)

When you write Authority tweets, lean on these **content types** (add your social proof & results when you're ready):

| Type | What it is | Example angle |
|------|------------|----------------|
| **Process** | Your methodology, steps, how you do it | "How I structure my writing day" |
| **Strategies** | Tactics that work (list format) | "Top 1% writers: [list]" — polarize, network, edit later, write first, concise, cut fluff, bullet points, white space, write as they speak, active voice |
| **Social proof** | Testimonials, client count, free value that led to proof | "I did [X] free [calls/essays]. → Collect testimonials, find pain points, overdeliver for free." |
| **Case studies** | One story, one outcome (before/after) | Client or your own journey in one thread |
| **Deep dives** | One topic, full breakdown | Long-form on one strategy or insight |
| **Niche insights** | Counter-intuitive truth + your numbers | "Bios are overrated. My profile conversion doubled when I started writing long-form (same bio). Focus on quality content." + [screenshot of metrics] |
| **Proof** | Revenue, followers, metrics + short story | "I hit $[X]/mo in less than [Y] months. • Zero days off • Countless mistakes • Multiple failed offers. Competence converts." + [dashboard/screenshot] |
| **How-to's / advice** | Practical, actionable | "How to [X] on X in 5 steps" |
| **Showing competence** | Demonstrate skill in the tweet itself | Tight writing, clear structure, no fluff |

*Tip: Use slides/screenshots for image tweets (e.g. metrics, revenue, testimonials) when you have the proof.*
