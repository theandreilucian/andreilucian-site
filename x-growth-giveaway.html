📊 GAP Framework Strategy Guide

The GAP Framework is used by top creators like Dakota Robertson, Dan Koe, Stijn Noorman, and many other top players on X.

Why is it important? Because it structures your content to maximize both reach and connection. Most creators post randomly and wonder why their growth is inconsistent. The GAP Framework solves this by giving you a clear system for what to post and when.

🌱 GROWTH

Things that work on the platform. Easy to get views, likes, and comments.

Short, 1-4 line tweets about mindset, growth, and universal truths that resonate with everyone. These are your high-engagement posts that get the algorithm working for you.

🎯 AUTHORITY

About you. About your niche. About your experience. Business tips.

Long-form deep dives on specific issues in your niche. This is where you establish yourself as an expert. Share your knowledge, experience, and tactical advice. This builds trust and positions you as the go-to person in your space.

💭 PERSONALITY

Life journey. Wins. Fails.

Medium-form posts about your life journey, wins, and failures. This is where people connect with you on a human level. Share your struggles, victories, and lessons learned. This builds the "know, like, trust" factor that turns followers into customers.

The framework works because it balances what the algorithm wants (Growth) with what builds your authority (Authority) and what makes people care about you (Personality). Top creators use this exact structure because it's proven to work.
