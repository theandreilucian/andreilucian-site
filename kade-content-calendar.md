## POSTING BEST PRACTICES

### Hook Formulas:
1. **Contrarian statement:** "Most people think X. I think Y."
2. **Personal story:** "I spent 2 years doing X. Here's what I learned."
3. **Bold claim:** "I run 6 companies. Here's how."
4. **Question:** "Why do most entrepreneurs fail at X?"
5. **Number + promise:** "5 systems that changed everything"

### Structure:
- **Hook** (first line - make them stop scrolling)
- **Value** (the meat - actionable insight)
- **Proof** (real numbers, examples, results)
- **CTA** (question, follow, save)

### Engagement Drivers:
- Ask questions
- Share vulnerable moments
- Provide actionable steps
- Use specific numbers
- Tell transformation stories
- Challenge common beliefs

### CTAs:
- "What's your experience?"
- "Save this post"
- "Follow for more [topic]"
- "Comment with [question]"
- "Repost if this resonates"
- "What would you add?"
