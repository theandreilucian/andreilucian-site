Introduction

Frontier is the Cray EX supercomputer with AMD MI250X GPUs at Oak Ridge (OLCF). Max HPL 1.353 EFLOPS (Nov 2025); debut 1.102 EFLOPS (June 2022). This article covers scale, node architecture, Slingshot dragonfly network, and Orion Lustre storage.

System overview

9,856 EX235a nodes; 9,856 Trento (Epyc 7A53); 39,424 MI250X (4 per node, 8 logical GCDs); 77 cabinets; Slingshot fabric, dragonfly topology.

Node architecture

Per EX235a: 1× Trento (64 Zen 3, 2.0–3.7 GHz); 512 GB DDR4 (205 GB/s); 2× 2 TB NVMe; 4× MI250X (128 GB HBM2e each, 3.2 TB/s/GPU); 4× Slingshot-11 NICs. On-node: Infinity Fabric GPU–GPU 50+50 GB/s, CPU–GPU 36+36; PCIe Gen4 ESM 50+50; Ethernet 25+25. Each physical MI250X = 2 logical GPUs (GCD).

Network architecture

One Cray EX4000 cabinet = one dragonfly group. 128 nodes, 32× 64-port 200G Slingshot, 4 NICs/node. Per switch: 16 L0, 31 L1, 16 L2. 4 links/group to every other group; blocking ≈ 1.753. Injection 102.4 Tb/s, ejection 58.4 Tb/s per group.

Storage — Orion

Orion: Lustre on Cray ClusterStor E1000, same dragonfly. 2 mgmt, 2 MGS, 40 MDS, 450 OSS, 160 LNET routers, 80 Slingshot, 35 mgmt switches. SSU: E1000, 24× 3.84 TB NVMe, 2 nodes (Rome 32c, 256 GB, 2× Slingshot-11), 212× 18 TB HDD. SSC = one dragonfly group: 45 SSUs, 32 LNET, 8 MDS (30.72 TB NVMe), 5 mgmt, 16 Slingshot. Five SSCs. OSTs: 5,400× 3.84 TB NVMe (11,400 TB formatted) + 47,700× 18 TB HDD (667,600 TB formatted). MDTs: 480× 30.72 TB NVMe (9,700 TB DoM). ZFS, dRAID.
