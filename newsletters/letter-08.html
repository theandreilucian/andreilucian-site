The specificity ladder

Climb the specificity ladder

Level 1: "post more" — invisible. Level 2: "post 3x/day" — slightly better. Level 3: "3x/day: story, lesson, proof" — useful. Level 4: numbers + failure + timeline — shareable.

Write like a witness, not a commentator.

De-genericize in 10 minutes

AI will keep flooding the feed with Level 1 and 2 content. Your job is Level 4 — witness writing with receipts. That's not harder. It's more honest.

Rewrite one old post by adding: one number, one failure, one constraint. Watch the difference in replies.

  • Highlight every sentence that could be anyone — delete or replace
  • Add one measured number
  • Add one thing that didn't work
  • Read aloud — if you cringe, you're closer

I grew 5.3K on 𝕏 and 16.2K on LinkedIn by climbing this ladder — not by being smarter.
