Two platforms. Two games. One idea.

Same core idea: "I tracked inputs for 30 days instead of checking followers." Two formats. Two completely different responses.

4 lines. Punchy. Number in line 1. Failure in line 3. 6,200 impressions. 41 replies. Fast feedback.

Master one game before you argue about the other.

What I'd do differently

I'd start LinkedIn 6 months earlier. Same voice, different packaging. I lost time being a 𝕏-only purist while my proof was sitting in a notes app.

14-day immersion

Pick the platform you're worst at. Post daily for 14 days. Steal structures from 10 accounts. Don't take a course — take notes.

Don't cross-post identical text. Same lesson, different packaging. 𝕏 wants compression. LinkedIn wants room to breathe. Respect the room.

If you're at zero on one platform, 14 days of daily posts will teach you more than any "ultimate guide" PDF.

  • Days 1–2: study only
  • Days 3–10: post daily
  • Days 11–13: double engagement
  • Day 14: keep or kill based on data
