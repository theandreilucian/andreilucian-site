The apology thread I never posted

Week 11 at 200 followers. I drafted: "Taking a short break to recalibrate." Classic prelude to quitting. I read it the next morning and felt embarrassed — not because taking breaks is wrong, but because I was asking permission to stop from an audience that wasn't watching anyway.

I deleted the draft. Posted a normal Tuesday. 3 replies turned into 1 DM. Small. The kind of small that compounds if you don't reset the clock.

Newsletter at 300, not 3,000

I waited too long to start the newsletter because I thought I needed scale first. Wrong order. Fifty subscribers who chose you at 300 followers are worth more than five hundred who followed a lead magnet at 10K. They knew your voice before the inbox — they weren't tricked by a hook.

Care compounds earlier than you think. Start ugly. Start at 50. Practice writing for humans who already decided you're worth their Tuesday.

The winners folder

Start the winners folder this week. Every post that gets a reply worth saving — screenshot. Every DM that makes you think — screenshot. By 1K you'll have a style guide written by the market, not by a course.

Comparison is theft with a timeline

At 200 followers I compared myself to accounts with five-year head starts. That's not competition — that's theft with a timeline. You're stealing their year-5 expectations for your year-0 reality.

The only fair comparison is inputs: are you replying more this month than last? Are your posts more specific? Are you closer to lens mode than repository mode? Those comparisons help. Follower comparisons hurt.

Write that down for future you. Laminate it if you have to. Stupid works.
