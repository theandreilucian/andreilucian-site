🧵 Here's what it is:

[Tweet 2/8] - BODY TWEET 1
The metric: Engagement Rate

Not total engagement.
Not follower count.
Not views.

Engagement RATE.

Formula:
(Total Engagements / Followers) × 100 = Engagement Rate

Example:
100 engagements ÷ 1,000 followers = 10% engagement rate

This tells you how much your audience actually cares.

[Tweet 3/8] - BODY TWEET 2
Why this matters:

High engagement rate = Strong connection
Low engagement rate = Weak connection

An account with:
• 10K followers, 2% engagement = 200 people care
• 1K followers, 20% engagement = 200 people care

Same impact. Different numbers.

Focus on the rate, not the total.

[Tweet 4/8] - BODY TWEET 3
What good looks like:

• 5%+ engagement rate = Excellent
• 3-5% engagement rate = Good
• 1-3% engagement rate = Average
• Below 1% engagement rate = Needs work

Most accounts hover around 1-2%.
The top accounts hit 5%+ consistently.

[Tweet 5/8] - BODY TWEET 4
How to improve your engagement rate:

1. Post content your audience actually wants
2. Ask questions that invite responses
3. Share personal stories that resonate
4. Provide value that's worth saving
5. Engage with every comment personally
6. Post at times when your audience is active
7. Use formats that encourage interaction

It's not about posting more.
It's about posting better.

[Tweet 6/8] - BODY TWEET 5
The trap to avoid:

Don't chase follower count at the expense of engagement rate.

Better to have:
• 1,000 followers, 10% engagement rate
Than:
• 10,000 followers, 1% engagement rate

Quality > Quantity
Always.
