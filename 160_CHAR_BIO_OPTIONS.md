## CHARACTER COUNT TIPS

### Space-Saving Techniques Used:

1. **Abbreviations:**
   - "4-fig" instead of "4-figure"
   - "DMs" instead of "direct messages"
   - "Content + engagement" instead of "content and engagement"

2. **Symbols:**
   - "→" instead of "which leads to" or "to"
   - "🔗" emoji saves space vs "Website:"
   - "|" instead of "and"

3. **Concise Language:**
   - "done-for-you" instead of "completely handled for you"
   - "served" instead of "founders served"
   - "getting leads" instead of "consistently generating inbound leads"

4. **Line Breaks:**
   - Strategic use of line breaks for readability
   - Each line has a purpose
   - Visual hierarchy maintained
