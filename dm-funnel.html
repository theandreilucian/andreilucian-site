How this closes faster:
  • Pre-pitch DMs make them say the pain. You only pitch warm people.
  • One CTA when you pitch. Never stack page + price + call + coaching in one message.
  • Follow-ups are courtesy, not a chase. No questions. No “are you interested.” No need to reply.
  • The second they ask how / price / help — jump to the pitch. Do not finish the sequence.
