## 3. Reels Strategy (Main growth lever)

**Length:** 15–30 seconds best for new accounts (higher completion rate). Up to 60s if it’s a full mini-workout.

**Structure:**
- **0–3 sec:** Hook (result, mistake, question, or “stop scrolling” moment).
- **3–20 sec:** One clear tip, one move, or one calorie fact.
- **Last 2–3 sec:** CTA — "Save ➡️ Share ➡️ Execute" on screen + in caption (emulate top accounts). Shares/DM sends = strongest signal for Explore.

**Reel ideas (use your existing footage):**

**Aerobic / fitness:**
- “30 sec cardio burst” (you doing the move).
- “Form check: don’t do this / do this” (before–after or side-by-side).
- “One move that burns the most in 1 min.”
- “What my aerobic class looks like” (short class highlight).
- “Cool-down stretch in 20 sec.”
- Trending audio + you doing a simple routine (same move on beat).

**Food / calories:**
- “How many calories in this?” (show meal, then number).
- “Full day of eating — calories included.”
- “This vs that — same calories, different volume.”
- “3 snacks under 150 kcal.”
- “Myth: X has way more calories than you think.”

**CapCut workflow for Reels:**
1. Import clip → trim to 15–30s.
2. Add **text overlay** (big, readable): key number or one line (e.g. “327 kcal” or “Do this 30 sec”).
3. Add **captions** (auto or manual) so it works with sound off.
4. Use **trending sounds** from Instagram (Reels Explore → save sound → use in CapCut or in-app).
5. Export 1080×1920 (9:16), max 60s.
