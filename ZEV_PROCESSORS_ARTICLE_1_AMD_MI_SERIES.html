Introduction

The Processors module is where hardware meets the stack: GPUs and APUs that drive training and inference at scale.

This article covers three AMD parts: MI250X (CDNA 2), MI300A (APU), and MI300X (CDNA 3)—from dual-GCD CDNA 2 to unified CDNA 3 and to a CPU–GPU APU.

AMD MI250X — CDNA 2, dual-GCD

Aug 11, 2024 · #GPU. MI250X is AMD’s high-end CDNA 2 GPU.

[Image: AMD MI250X]
Product or block diagram

One MI250X package = two GCDs over Infinity Fabric on one OAM package. To the OS it appears as two GPUs.

Per package:

  • 2 GCDs → 220 CUs, 14,080 stream processors, 880 matrix cores
  • 1.7 GHz peak; no sparsity
  • 128 GB HBM2e, 3.2 TB/s
  • 8× Infinity Fabric D2D, 1×16 PCIe Gen4; 560 W max

Peak TFLOPS: FP64 VFMA 47.9 / Matrix 95.7; FP32 same; FP16/BF16/INT8/INT4 Matrix 383.

AMD MI300A — APU (CPU + GPU)

Nov 29, 2024 · #GPU #CPU. MI300A is AMD’s APU: one Zen 4 CPU tile + three CDNA 3 GPU tiles. “Replace 25% of an MI300X with a Genoa CPU.”

[Image: AMD MI300A]
Product or block diagram

CPU: 24 Zen 4 cores @ 3.7 GHz.

GPU: 6 XCDs, 228 CUs, 14,592 stream processors, 912 matrix cores; 2.1 GHz; 4:2 structured sparsity; 128 GB HBM3, 5.3 TB/s; 8×16 Infinity Fabric, PCIe Gen5; 760 W max.

Peak (GPU): FP64 61.3; FP32 122.6; TF32/FP16/BF16/FP8/INT8 Matrix and Sparse (e.g. FP8/INT8 Sparse 3920).

APUs matter for tight CPU–GPU coupling and a single memory space—simulation + inference, or orchestration and model on the same socket.

AMD MI300X — CDNA 3, 192 GB HBM3

Jun 19, 2025 · #GPU. MI300X is AMD’s first CDNA 3 GPU: big memory, high FLOPs for frontier training and large-model inference.

Why this lineup matters

MI250X — CDNA 2, dual-GCD, 128 GB HBM2e. Proven for HPC and AI before CDNA 3.

MI300A — APU. Zen 4 + CDNA 3 in one package. Integrated CPU–GPU, simpler system design.

MI300X — CDNA 3, 192 GB HBM3, 1.2k+ matrix cores. Frontier training and hosting very large models (e.g. 405B FP16) in one 8-way group.

Sparsity (2:4 and 4:2) and matrix-core counts are where AMD is pushing efficiency and peak TFLOPS for AI. For the Processors module, these three are the ones to know.
