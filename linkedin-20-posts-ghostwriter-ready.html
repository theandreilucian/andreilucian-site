Post 3 — Education (Wed) — Template 11: Give a Hack

One hack that changed how I write online:

Write like you're talking to one person on a call.

Not "Dear audience." Not "Hey everyone."
One person. Their problem. Your take. One clear idea per post.

When you write for everyone, you connect with no one.
When you write for one person — the one who's stuck, failing, or ready to grow — you attract the right people.

I still imagine one founder or creator when I write.
It keeps my writing clear, human, and useful.
