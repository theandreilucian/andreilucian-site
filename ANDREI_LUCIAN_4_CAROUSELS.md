## Carousel 1 — 5 Slides That Get Your LinkedIn Posts Read

**Topic:** LinkedIn / writing / hooks  
**Caption idea:** "Most posts die at the first line. Here are 5 that don't."

---

**Slide 1 (Title)**  
**5 Slides That Get Your LinkedIn Posts Read**

---

**Slide 2**  
**1. Lead with a hook, not a hello**  
First line = curiosity or tension.  
No "Hope you're well" or "Quick thought."

---

**Slide 3**  
**2. One idea per post**  
One problem. One takeaway. One CTA.  
Clarity beats clever.

---

**Slide 4**  
**3. Short lines, white space**  
2–3 sentences max per block.  
Easier to scan = more reads.

---

**Slide 5**  
**4. End with a question**  
Specific, easy to answer.  
"What's your biggest challenge with [X]?"  
Not "What do you think?"

---

**Slide 6**  
**5. Reply to every comment**  
Within 1–2 hours when you can.  
Algorithm rewards conversation.

---
