**Questions to Answer:**

### Posting Frequency

## 3. CONTENT QUALITY ANALYSIS

### Hook Analysis
Pick their top 5-10 performing tweets and analyze:

**Hook Types:**
- [ ] Pattern interrupts ("Stop X, start Y")
- [ ] Numbers ("10 things...", "90% of...")
- [ ] Contrarian takes ("Everyone says X, but...")
- [ ] Curiosity gaps ("The mistake most people make...")
- [ ] Personal stories ("I used to...")

**Document:**
```
Hook Type: [Type]
Example: "[Hook text]"
Why it works: [Analysis]
```

### Thread Structure
Analyze 3-5 of their best threads:

**Structure Analysis:**
- [ ] How many tweets in thread?
- [ ] What's the hook (Tweet 1)?
- [ ] How do they structure the content?
- [ ] Do they use numbers/steps?
- [ ] What's the CTA (final tweet)?

**Document:**
```
Thread Topic: [Topic]
Length: [X tweets]
Structure:
- Tweet 1: [Hook]
- Tweets 2-3: [Setup]
- Tweets 4-8: [Content]
- Final: [CTA]
```

### Value Delivery
- [ ] Do they provide actionable frameworks?
- [ ] Do they share specific examples?
- [ ] Do they include proof/numbers?
- [ ] Is content educational or entertainment?

**Questions:**
- What value do they provide?
- Why would someone follow them?
- What makes their content shareable?
