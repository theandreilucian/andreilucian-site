GAP Framework One-Pager

Structure: 30 Growth | 30 Personality | 30 Authority. Use this to plan your week so you're not posting the same thing every day.

G — Growth

Goal: Follower growth, reach, saves, shares. Clear value and hooks.

Templates to lean on: Comparison/Opposites, List Tweets, Hook-List-Takeaway, Rule of 3, Repetition List, Stop X Start Y, Challenging Common Beliefs, Powerful One-Liner.

Topics to tie in: High-income skills, goals, opportunity cost, simplicity, personal responsibility, freedom, you are your biggest competitor.

What to post: Short, 1–4 line tweets about mindset & growth. List tweets, Stop X Start Y, comparison, one-liners.

A — Authority

Goal: Prove expertise. X growth, writing, your numbers and outcomes.

Templates to lean on: 2×3 Comparison (before/after results), Challenging Common Beliefs, The Callout, Hook-List-Takeaway (how you win), Short Personal Story (your journey), Comparison/Opposites (myth vs your method).

Topics to tie in: I hit X followers, I made my first money, high-income skills, writing, simplicity, opportunity cost.

Content types: Process (your methodology), Strategies (tactics that work, list format), Social proof (testimonials, client count), Case studies (one story, one outcome), Deep dives (one topic, full breakdown), Niche insights (counter-intuitive truth + your numbers), Proof (revenue, followers, metrics), How-to's, Showing competence in the tweet itself.

P — Personality

Goal: Likeability, relatability, mindset. Motivation, discipline, health, learning.

Templates to lean on: Short Personal Story, Rule of 3, The Callout, Stop X Start Y, Repetition List (Replace X with Y), Metaphor, Powerful One-Liner.

Topics to tie in: Acknowledging weakness, failure → success, perfectionism, personal responsibility, talking about goals, simplicity, freedom.

Content types: Ideas (frameworks, reframes, one big idea + short list), Stories (past → present, lesson at the end), Transformations (before/after in list form, punchy closer), Milestones (hit X followers / first $ / goal + reflection), Values (your rules, principles, non-negotiables), Behind the scenes (what you're building + photo), Goals / building in public (public goal + progress), Lifestyle (what you love about the creator life — time, freedom).

Your split: 30 Growth · 30 Personality · 30 Authority. Total: 90 tweets. Adjust counts to taste; keep the balance.
