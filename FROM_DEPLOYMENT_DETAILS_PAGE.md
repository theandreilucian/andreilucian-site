# 📍 You're on a Deployment Details Page

## ✅ What to Do From Here:

### Option 1: Redeploy This Deployment (Easiest)

1. **Look at the TOP RIGHT** of this page
2. **Find the "Visit" button** (it has a dropdown arrow)
3. **Click the dropdown arrow** next to "Visit"
4. **Look for "Redeploy"** in the menu
5. **Click "Redeploy"**
6. **Wait 1-2 minutes** for it to build

---

### Option 2: Go Back to Deployments List

1. **Look at the TOP navigation bar**
2. **Click "Deployments"** (the tab, not the back button)
3. **You'll see the list of all deployments**
4. **Then follow the previous instructions** to redeploy

---

### Option 3: Check if There's a Redeploy Button

1. **Scroll down** on this page
2. **Look for a "Redeploy" button** anywhere on the page
3. **Click it** if you find it

---

## 🎯 Quickest Way:

**Try this first:**
1. Click the **dropdown arrow** next to "Visit" button (top right)
2. Look for **"Redeploy"** option
3. Click it
4. Wait 1-2 minutes

**If that doesn't work:**
1. Click **"Deployments"** in the top nav bar
2. Go back to the deployments list
3. Click "..." on the top deployment
4. Click "Redeploy"
