How to land your first paid client

You don’t need 10K. You need 1,000, proof, and 5 people who’ll say your name.

Skip the funnel. Skip the webinar. Run this.

1. Grow to 1,000 as fast as you can

Personal brand. Your face. Your name.

10 templates. 30 comments/day. 5 DMs. Reply to all.

Speed matters. Invisible accounts don’t get hired.

2. Good content

One idea. One number if you have it. White space. A closer.

If they can’t finish it, they won’t pay you.

3. Good engagement

Sit on your posts. Reply to everyone.

Comment like a mini-post on other people.

DMs after they already talked. Never “let’s connect.”

4. Work for free

Pick 3–5 people you’d be proud to put on a page.

Do the job properly. On time. In their voice.

You’re not cheap. You’re collecting receipts.

5. Collect 5 testimonials

When the work is done, ask:

Ask
Glad it shipped. Could you write 3–4 lines I can use? • What was broken • What we did • What changed A screenshot works.

No testimonial = you don’t move to paid yet. Get five.

6. Find paid clients two ways

Pin a testimonial. Then hunt. Inbound and outbound. Every day.

Inbound — they come to you

From your posts, comments, and DMs they start.

Who: people who reply, quote you, or already messaged.

You sit 60 min. You answer everyone. You DM the ones with a real problem.

Inbound DM
Saw your comment on [X]. I helped [name] go [result]. They said: [1 line] I write. You approve. I ship. Want a spot?

Outbound — you go to them

Open other people’s posts in your niche. Read the comments.

Look for accounts that:

  • Have an offer (coach, agency, founder, writer)
  • Posting is dead or messy
  • Bio doesn’t match the tweets
  • They’re asking how to grow / get clients

Click the profile. 30 seconds: bio, pin, last 5 posts. Then reach out. Comment first, then DM.

Outbound DM
[Name] — Looked at [their last post / pin]. What's working: [1 line] What's leaking: [bio / pin / you post and leave] I did this for [name] → [result]. Happy to send 3 hooks. No pitch unless you ask.

5 outbound DMs a day. Same as the growth loop. Proof in message 2, not message 1.

1,000. Good posts. Real replies. Free work. 5 testimonials. Then inbound + outbound every day. That's it.
