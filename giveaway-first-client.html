How to land your first client from X

Not a funnel. A post they finish + a DM after they talk.

The lie: you need 10K followers. The truth: I had $223 in month 3. Iordan got $100 writing posts. George got his first coaching client from the content.

The loop

  1. Post proof (numbers, scar, or “I do this for people”)
  2. Sit 60 min. Reply like a human.
  3. DM only people who commented a real problem.
  4. Help first. Offer last.

Posts that start the conversation

  • Wall tweets → I stuck with it → Don’t quit
  • 3 / 50 / 20 → 3,000 → 4,380
  • $0 / $0 / $223 / $7,502
  • “I write. You stay in the business. You approve. I publish.”

Who to DM

Commented “nobody replies” / “views are dead” / “how do I get clients?”

Skip: “me” / “interested” / fire emoji.

DM 1
I saw [their stuck]. I was there. What are you posting, and how long?
DM 2
Here's what I'd do at your number. 4 posts. Sit 60 min. List + question at 11:30. Want me to look at 3 posts?
DM 3
You're missing [hook / proof / sitting]. Fix that for 14 days.
DM 4 — offer last
If you want the daily system, it's in my profile. If you want me to write the account, I ghostwrite one channel. Either is fine. Don't quit.

What you’re selling (pick one)

  • You write for them (ghostwrite)
  • You coach the quotas
  • You sell a simple system

One offer. In the last DM. Not the first.

Don’t wait for 10K. Talk to the people already talking to you.
