TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Start with the base prompt, then add specific elements
2. Use "symbolic of [concept]" to guide the AI
3. Specify "white background" for clean carousel images
4. Use "detailed line work" for that engraving aesthetic
5. Add "high contrast" for strong black and white
6. For triptychs, specify "three panels" or "triptych format"
7. Reference classic engravers for authentic style
8. Iterate - generate multiple versions and pick the best
9. Use negative prompts: "no color, no gradients, no shadows"
10. For Instagram, ensure images are square (1:1) or vertical (4:5)
