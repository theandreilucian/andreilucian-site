How to DM on X

Short. Real. No pitch in message 1.

Only DM people you actually like. Comment on them first. If they ignore you, move on. That’s it.

Never send

  • “Let’s connect”
  • “Hi” / “How are you?”
  • Ask for a follow or RT
  • “Let’s grow together”
  • A novel. Or a voice note.
  • A pitch in the first message

That’s needy. They delete it.

The loop

  1. Find them in the comments (your niche).
  2. Leave 2–3 real comments before you DM.
  3. Message 1: one specific compliment + one question.
  4. If they talk: ask what they’re building. Listen.
  5. If you can help, help. Don’t ask what they want. Don’t ask for anything back.

If it doesn’t click, stop. You don’t need 100 friends. You need a few.

01 · First DM
[Name] — Saw [their post / bio]. [One sentence why it hit — your life, not “great content.”] [One specific question]
02 · They reply
Got it. What are you building right now? [Optional: one thing you’d try at their size]
03 · You can help
I noticed [their problem]. I’d do [one free, specific thing]. No catch. Use it or don’t.

Bigger accounts

Same rules. More value first. They’re busy, not gods.

Never ask them to follow you. Never get mad if they skip you. Give. Walk away.

Fill the brackets. Keep it short. One follow-up, then stop. Don't quit.
