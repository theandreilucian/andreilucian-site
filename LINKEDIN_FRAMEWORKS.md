## Top Frameworks

### 1. Justin Welsh's Hook → Value → Engagement Framework

**Structure:**
```
HOOK (Line 1):
- Contrarian statement
- Bold claim
- Problem statement
- "Most people [wrong belief]. Here's why..."

VALUE (Lines 2-10):
- Specific framework
- Step-by-step process
- Real example/case study
- Actionable insight

ENGAGEMENT (End):
- Specific question (not "What do you think?")
- "What's your biggest challenge with [topic]?"
- "What's been your experience with [topic]?"
```

**Example:**
```
Most creators waste 2 years posting with no strategy.

Here's the framework that works:

STEP 1: Hook with a Contrarian Statement
Start with something that challenges common belief.

STEP 2: Provide Value in the Middle
Give them something actionable they can use today.

STEP 3: End with Engagement
Ask a question that makes them want to comment.

The formula: Hook → Value → Engagement

What's your biggest challenge with LinkedIn growth?
```

### 2. Dan Koe's Authority Framework

**Structure:**
```
Line 1: Hook (problem or insight)
Lines 2-4: Context (why this matters)
Lines 5-10: Deep value (framework, system, process)
Line 11: Question (invite discussion)
```

**Example:**
```
Most founders struggle with personal branding because they lead with credentials instead of problems.

I've worked with 20+ founders building authority online.

The shift that changed everything:

From: "I'm a ghostwriter who helps founders write content"
To: "I help founders who struggle with consistency build authority through strategic content"

The psychology: Problems are more relatable than credentials.

When you lead with the problem your audience faces, you attract the right people.

What's your approach to personal branding?
```

### 3. Dickie Bush's Story Framework

**Structure:**
```
1. Setup (the situation)
2. Struggle (the problem)
3. Solution (what worked)
4. Success (the result)
5. Lesson (what you learned)
```

```
SOLUTION:
I focused on one thing: providing value through frameworks and insights.

SUCCESS:
In just 6 months, I grew from zero to over 20,000 followers.

LESSON:
The difference wasn't more posting. It was better positioning.

What's your biggest challenge with LinkedIn growth?
```

## Posting Frequency: 3-5 Posts Per Week

**Why 3-5 posts/week is optimal:**
- Quality over quantity
- Allows time for engagement
- Prevents burnout
- Better algorithm performance
- More sustainable long-term

**Best Posting Times:**
- 8-9 AM (morning commute)
- 12-1 PM (lunch break)
- 5-6 PM (evening scroll)

---

## Content Mix (Weekly)

**Recommended Distribution:**
- 40% Framework/Educational posts
- 30% Personal stories/Case studies
- 20% Industry insights
- 10% Behind-the-scenes

**GAP Framework Application:**
- **Growth (30%):** Frameworks, motivation, inspiration
- **Authority (30%):** Results, expertise, case studies
- **Personality (40%):** Personal stories, travel, work behind scenes

---

## Engagement Strategy

**Daily Engagement:**
- 50 comments per day
- 10-15 DMs per day
- Reply to ALL comments on your posts (within 1 hour)
- Engage in 2-3 groups daily

**Engagement Tiers:**
- Tier 1 (50K+ followers): 5 comments
- Tier 2 (10K-50K): 15 comments
- Tier 3 (1K-10K): 10 comments
- Your posts: Reply to all

---

## Native Features to Use

**Weekly Mix:**
- **Polls:** 2-3 per week (easy engagement)
- **Documents:** 2-3 per week (carousel-style content)
- **Articles:** 1 per week (long-form, knowledge-dense)
- **Live Sessions:** 1 per month (Q&A)

---

## Key Principles

1. **Structure > Content:** How you say it matters more than what you say
2. **Hook First:** First line determines if they read the rest
3. **Value Always:** Every post must provide actionable value
4. **Engage Always:** End with a question that invites discussion
5. **Consistency > Frequency:** 3-5 quality posts > 7 mediocre posts
6. **Authenticity Wins:** Share real struggles, not just wins
7. **Reply Fast:** Respond to comments within 1 hour (algorithm boost)
