Generating high-performing ideas

Virality isn't the goal at 0→1K — consistent engagement is. But it would be silly not to put effort into making every post land harder.

How do you make even a boring topic engaging? We go deeper in Module 5 (workflow), but here's the strategy I used for years:

The secret (especially at the start): DO WHAT WORKS. That's why imitation and emulation matter. Don't invent new formats on day 3.

Where to find content ideas

  • Most popular YouTube videos from creators in your niche
  • Top-ranking blog posts (Google your Domain of Mastery topics)
  • Featured articles on Medium in your interests
  • Top tweets from niche creators (X search, Twemex, or TweetHunter)
  • Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok — high engagement = steal the idea, not the words

Pull search topics from your Domain of Mastery — that's your filter for YouTube, Google, and Medium.

Original ideas that still perform

When you want perspectives only you can write:

  • Read books · listen to podcasts & audiobooks
  • Long YouTube lectures (YT Premium = listen with screen off)
  • Blinkist or Shortform summaries for fast pattern intake

Then plug those ideas into a structure that already performs (see below). Curiosity + proven format = your edge.

Turning ideas into posts

Content you find online is tried and true — especially YouTube titles. Your job: take the big idea and make it yours under your brand.

From a headline or title

  • Short-form — single tweet, hook, or list post
  • Expansion — one thought triggers another; write your angle

From a video, podcast, or article

  • List tweets — if the source is numbered, remix the structure
  • Knowledge bombs — one insight per tweet
  • Threads — outline already exists; synthesize in your voice
Don't plagiarize — synthesize. Everyone remixes ideas. Your bias, story, and niche make it unique. There's only so much to say on any topic — say the versions that work, in your voice.

Capture ideas while you consume

  1. Consume using the sources above
  2. Keep Notion (or your notes app) open on your phone
  3. Capture sparks immediately — hook, headline, one sentence
  4. When you batch (Module 5), plug each idea into a structure below or a GAP template

That's how you balance original curiosity with proven performance.

Tweet structures to emulate

Imitate, then innovate. Too many beginners invent their own format on week one — then blame the algorithm. My advice: do what works, with your ideas.

Core structures (1–11)

Structure 1

Commentary On Objections

React to advice you hear — your inner dialogue becomes the post.
Everyone says "post more." Nobody says post *better*. Volume without hooks is noise.
Structure 2

Extreme Advice

Bold take + conviction. No curse needed — energy comes from word choice.
Stop treating X like a diary. Treat it like a billboard. One idea. One punch. One reason to follow.
Structure 3

Metaphors

Explain your idea through a comparison people already understand.
Growing on X is like going to the gym: • Month 1: nobody notices • Month 3: you notice • Month 6: they notice Consistency is the rep.
Structure 4

Personal Definitions

Define a word your way. Fast to write, high save rate.
Focus isn't discipline. Focus is saying no to good ideas so the great one can breathe.
Structure 5

Repetitive Advice

One sentence of advice → brainstorm variations on the theme.
Show up daily. Show up when it's boring. Show up when nobody claps. That's the whole game.
Structure 6

This Comes After This

Chain ideas like a story — one word leads to the next.
Curiosity → click → profile → follow → trust → offer Miss step 1 and nothing else matters.
Structure 7

If This, Then That

Start with "if" — conditional logic that feels inevitable.
If you reply to 30 accounts/day for 90 days, then growth isn't a mystery anymore. It's math.
Structure 8

Harsh Truth + Hook

Warning hook → uncomfortable truth → path forward.
Nobody's coming to save your follower count. Not the algorithm. Not a course. Not luck. Daily reps. That's it.
Structure 9

Your Way Of Doing X

How YOU do something common — observant, specific.
My content batch: • Sunday 90 min • 14 posts queued • Zero writing Mon–Fri Protect the batch. Protect the growth.
Structure 10

Polarity & Repetition

Compare opposites or nuanced contrasts.
Consumers scroll. Creators study. Same app. Different outcome.
Structure 11

Unique Hack

A hack you found or discovered in real life.
My laptop died at a coffee shop. Forced 45-min sprint. Best work week I had. Now I leave it unplugged on purpose.

Advanced structures (12–22)

Structure 12

List For Your Tribe

Traits of people you want to attract — they self-identify.
Creators who win on X: • Reply more than they post • Pin proof, not personality • Batch on Sundays • Remix winners, don't reinvent
Structure 13

Interesting Observation

Notice something. Say it simply. Optional hook on top.
The accounts stuck at 200 followers all have the same bio: No who. No what. No why. Fix the bio before you post again.
Structure 14

Subtweet Yourself

Advice you'd give yourself — positioned at others.
You're not "shadowbanned." You posted 4 times this week and replied twice. The algorithm didn't fail you. Consistency did.
Structure 15

2–3 Step Punchy Advice

Short list = solution to one problem.
Stuck below 500? 1. Fix bio today 2. 30 replies tomorrow 3. Batch 7 posts Sunday Repeat 12 weeks.
Structure 16

You Can… But You Have To

Desired outcome + requirements. Numbers help.
You can hit 1K in 90 days. But you have to: • 2 posts/day • 30 replies/day • 1 thread/week No shortcuts. Just reps.
Structure 17

Problem → Solution

Name the cause. Name the fix.
Low impressions? Cause: weak hooks. Fix: study 10 top posts in your niche. Steal structures. Fill with your ideas.
Structure 18

Unique Angle On Common Problem

Everyone talks about X — give your spin.
Everyone says "be authentic." Authentic without structure is a diary. Structure without you is AI slop. You need both.
Structure 19

The Best & Worst

Think in extremes — entertaining and shareable.
Worst thing you can do at 200 followers: Post 5x/day with zero replies. Best thing: 2 posts + 40 thoughtful replies.
Structure 20

Join The Conversation

Reply spark → standalone post. Your take on what's trending.
Saw a thread: "Replies don't matter." They matter more under 1K. Replies ARE your distribution when you have no reach.
Structure 21

Problem In Your Life

What you're struggling with + how you'll solve it.
I'm rebuilding my queue from scratch. Plan: • 45 templates filled by Friday • Sunday batch locked • No posting without GAP tag Documenting it here.
Structure 22

Horizontal & Vertical Questions

Who/what/when/where/how/why — branch into new ideas.
Why do creators burn out? → They mix writing + publishing in one session. How to fix? → Capture Sunday. Publish all week.

More ways to find posts

Join the online conversation

  • Read a tweet you disagree with? Post your take — that's a structure.
  • Did a reply spark something? Turn the reply into a standalone post.
  • Your inner commentary while scrolling = unlimited drafts.

Identify problems in your own life

What are you struggling with right now? How will you solve it? Document publicly. Reflection fuels Personality posts.

Horizontal & vertical questioning

Branch ideas with: who, what, when, where, how, why.

Example chain:

Topic: strategy → Why do you need strategy? → To work efficiently → On what? → Content → How? → List: 5 ways to speed up content creation. That's a tweet.

Let thoughts trigger thoughts. Question until you hit something worth posting.
