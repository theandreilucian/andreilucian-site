Gumroad setup — The X System (ZIP delivery)
===========================================

FLOW
----
  Your landing page (andreilucian.com) → Gumroad checkout → email with ZIP

  Landing: .../0-to-1K-X-System/LANDING.html
  Buy buttons redirect to your Gumroad product URL (set in product-checkout.js)


STEP 1 — Build the ZIP (on your computer)
-----------------------------------------
  node scripts/build_product_zip.mjs

  Output: 0-to-1K-X-System/downloads/The-X-System.zip


STEP 2 — Create product on Gumroad
----------------------------------
  1. Gumroad → Products → New product
  2. Name: The X System
  3. Price: $47 USD (one-time)
  4. Description: copy from GUMROAD-SALES-PAGE.html or LANDING.html
  5. Cover: use x-growth-system-playbook-cover or product art
  6. Content → Upload file: downloads/The-X-System.zip
  7. Enable: "I will email my customers" (default — Gumroad sends receipt + download)


STEP 3 — Paste link on your site
--------------------------------
  Gumroad → Share → copy product link (andreilucian.gumroad.com/l/xxxxx)

  Open: 0-to-1K-X-System/product-checkout.js
  Replace:
    gumroadProductUrl: "https://andreilucian.gumroad.com/l/YOUR_X_SYSTEM_LINK"
  With your real link.


STEP 4 — Optional: add hub link in Gumroad email
------------------------------------------------
  In Gumroad product → Settings → Email confirmation, add:

  "Online hub (browser): https://andreilucian.com/0-to-1K-X-System/INDEX.html
   Folder guide: https://andreilucian.com/0-to-1K-X-System/OPEN-FIRST.html"

  Buyers get ZIP in email + can use the hub online anytime.


STEP 5 — Test
-------------
  1. Open LANDING.html → click Get The X System
  2. Complete test purchase on Gumroad
  3. Confirm email arrives with ZIP download
  4. Unzip → open OPEN-FIRST.html or INDEX.html inside


REBUILD ZIP AFTER UPDATES
-------------------------
  node scripts/build_product_zip.mjs
  Re-upload The-X-System.zip on Gumroad (Products → Content → replace file)
