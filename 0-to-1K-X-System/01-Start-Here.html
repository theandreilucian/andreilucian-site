How growth actually works

Three things matter under 1K:

  1. Your profile — people need to know who you are in 3 seconds
  2. Your posts — you need stuff to say (templates help)
  3. Your replies — commenting on other people gets you seen
Real talk: You won't grow by only posting. You need to comment on other people's posts too. Every day. 60 times. That's how people find you.

Stick with it for 90 days. If you do the daily checklist, 1,000 followers is realistic.
