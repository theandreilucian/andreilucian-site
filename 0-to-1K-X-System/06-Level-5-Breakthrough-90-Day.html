How to DM (without being weird)

Max 10 DMs per day. More than that looks spammy and X might limit you.

  1. Find someone your size doing cool stuff
  2. Send a real compliment — mention something specific they posted
  3. Ask what they're working on — don't pitch anything
  4. Share something helpful if you can — don't beg for a follow or RT
