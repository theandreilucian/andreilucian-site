Reply Bank

You need 60 comments per day. Copy these, change the words, send. Don't write "great post!" — say something real.

How to use

  1. Find posts from people on your 100 creator list
  2. Pick a template below that fits their post
  3. Fill in the [brackets] with your own words
  4. Send 60 per day — 30 morning, 30 evening
Rule: One good comment beats 20 lazy ones. But you still need volume — aim for 60/day.
