Day 1

Your First 24 Hours

Follow these steps today. Don't read the whole course. Just do this.

Fix my bio → Set 90-day start date

What you'll finish today

  • New bio live on X
  • 3 posts published (copy from templates)
  • 15 replies sent to other creators
  • 90-day timer started

Step 1 · Set up (15 min)

  1. Bookmark: Hub, Cheat Sheet, Daily Checklist
  2. Go to Daily Checklist → pick your 90-day start date
  3. On the Hub → type your follower count today

Step 2 · Fix your profile (30 min)

  1. Open Bio Builder → write your new bio
  2. Paste it on X + add a link (newsletter or free ebook)
  3. Run the Profile Checklist — fix anything that's off
  4. Pick what your pinned post will be about (you'll write it later)

Step 3 · Post 3 times (30 min)

  1. Open Post Templates
  2. Pick 3 templates — fill in the blanks (don't write from scratch)
  3. Post #1 right now — don't wait for perfect
  4. Save post #2 and #3 for later today or tomorrow

Step 4 · Comment on 15 posts (45 min)

  1. Open Reply Bank — copy a reply, make it yours
  2. Find creators in your niche on X
  3. Send 15 real comments — not "great post!" — add something useful
  4. Start your 100 creator list: 30 big · 30 medium · 40 your size

Step 5 · Save ideas (15 min)

  1. Make a simple Notion page or notes app — call it "Post Ideas"
  2. Write down 10 things you could post about
  3. Block 90 minutes on your calendar this Sunday — that's when you batch posts for the week

Day 1 done? Check these off

You're done for today. Tomorrow: open the Daily Checklist and follow it — 3–5 posts, 60 replies, 10 DMs. Same thing for 90 days.
