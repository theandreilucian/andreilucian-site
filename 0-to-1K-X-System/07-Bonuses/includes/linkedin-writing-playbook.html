Intro about LinkedIn

LinkedIn is the best platform for building authority, trust, and inbound opportunities if you use it with consistency.

This playbook is focused on one outcome: help you look credible, publish useful content, grow your network, and turn attention into opportunities.

We keep this simple: profile first, then content, then networking, then offer.

1. Profile Optimisation

Your profile should answer in 5 seconds:

  • Who you help
  • What result you help them get
  • Why they should trust you

Focus on headline, banner, profile photo, and about section. Keep your message specific and proof-based.

3. Networking

Networking on LinkedIn works best when you add value before asking for anything.

  • Build a target list of people in your niche
  • Comment meaningfully on their posts consistently
  • Connect after real interaction, not cold pitching
  • Reply to every quality interaction on your own posts

This builds trust, visibility, and warm inbound conversations over time.

12

Adding Industry Titans

Create a Target List

Who are 5-10 industry titans that you want to connect with? Put them in a Google Sheet and start the process below.

Step 1: Stay OUT of their inbox

There is nothing worse than getting a LinkedIn message or InMail from someone you have zero interaction with, and they are asking for a favor. Do not do this.

Step 2: Routinely interact with their content

To play it safe, respect people's time and respect their inbox. If they are a titan in your industry, it's likely they get 30-40 messages every single day. Be different by respecting their inbox.

Before asking them to connect, interact meaningfully with at least 10 pieces of their content. Meaningful interaction is a thoughtful, professional comment that furthers the discussion.

Most comments on LinkedIn are generic, so you'll stand out and have a better chance of being selected as "top comment". This will help get you noticed.

13

Networking — connection process

Step 3: Create a "solo recognition" post

This typically will get flagged by LinkedIn as spam and also won't win you any points with those titans if you make spammy posts tagging too many people.

I often see people on LinkedIn create a spammy post where they ask a question and then tag 20 titans in their industry.

Instead, when you find a post that really resonates with you, create your own post and call out that ONE titan. Tell them how much their post helped, and tag only them. This is the fastest way to a connection.

Step 4: Connect meaningfully

If you have stayed out of someone's inbox, interacted with their posts regularly, and created a "solo recognition" post, then it could be the right time to connect. When you send a connection, make it custom.

Audience Boosting Interactions

Use the "top" and "recent" sort function

The automatic sorting of your LinkedIn feed is set to "top" where you'll see top posts from your network. The "recent" view helps you find new posts early and increase your odds of becoming a top comment.

When you're one of the first thoughtful commenters, you can earn more likes, profile views, and followers from that thread.

Daily interaction baseline

Leave 5 thoughtful comments per day on people in your niche. Build a spreadsheet of creators you follow and comment before publishing your own post.

TIP: post visibility improves when your own post gets 30 interactions and 10 comments in the first hour.

5 comment examples

  • "Great point on {topic}. We tested similar and saw {result}. Have you seen this in {context}?"
  • "Strong take. Most people skip {step}. Your line on {specific line} explains why results stall."
  • "I like this framing of {idea}. For early-stage creators I'd add {practical step} first."
  • "Big takeaway: {one takeaway}. This alone can save months of trial and error."
  • "Agree on {point}. One respectful challenge: {counterpoint}. Curious on your view."

Audience Boosting Interactions — DM Playbook

5 DM opener examples

  • "Hey {name}, your post on {topic} was excellent — especially {detail}. Helped me a lot."
  • "{Name}, your {post/carousel} on {topic} gave me clarity on {pain point}. Thank you."
  • "Quick one — your point on {specific point} changed how I'm approaching {activity}."
  • "Loved your take on {topic}. I'm building in {niche}, and {detail} was super useful."
  • "Thanks for posting {title/topic}. Rare to see this much signal on LinkedIn."

DM funnel template (copy/paste)

1. Warm opener: "Hey {name}, enjoyed your post about {topic}. {detail} stood out."

2. Context: "I'm working with {audience} on {outcome}, solving {pain point}."

3. Micro-value: "One quick idea that might help: {insight}."

4. Permission question: "Open to a quick thought on improving {specific area}?"

5. Soft CTA: "If useful, happy to send a 3-point breakdown here — no pitch."

6. Optional call: "If relevant, we can do a quick 15-min call. No pressure."

Follow-up cadence

Day 0: opener

Day 3: value follow-up with one useful note

Day 7: final check-in

Then stop. Keep the relationship warm through comments.

4. Craft your offer

Positioning rule: A weak offer says "I do content." A strong offer says "I help {who} get {outcome} in {timeframe} with {mechanism}."

Offer building framework

Great offers are specific. If your offer is vague, people delay. If your offer is clear, people decide.

  • Who: exact audience
  • Outcome: measurable result
  • Mechanism: your process
  • Timeline: clear timeframe
  • Scope: included + excluded
  • Proof: case studies / testimonials
  • Guarantee: risk reversal
  • CTA: next action to start

Example 3 — Growth Sprint (Guarantee model)

Who: founders starting from scratch

Offer: Grow your X account from 0 to 1000 followers in 120 days, or money back.

Mechanism: profile optimization + daily content cadence + comment engine + DM funnel.

Minimum client actions required:

  • Post consistently for 120 days

Bonuses: My Pack Of Tools I Use

These are the core tools I use weekly to create content, run distribution, and monetize:

  • Hypefury — schedule posts, manage publishing consistency, and keep daily output organized.
  • qura.ai — generate smarter engagement ideas and comment angles faster.
  • Canva — design visuals, carousels, and simple branded assets quickly.
  • Notion — content database, pipeline tracking, and operating system for ideas.
  • ChatGPT — ideation, structure drafts, and first-pass rewrites for speed.
  • Gumroad — sell digital products and capture payments with simple checkout.
  • Cursor.ai — build pages, edit files, and move fast on technical tasks.
  • Kit — newsletter growth, subscriber management, and broadcast emails.
  • Hemingway — tighten writing clarity, reduce fluff, and improve readability.

Use fewer tools, but use them deeply. Stack them into one repeatable workflow and execute daily.
