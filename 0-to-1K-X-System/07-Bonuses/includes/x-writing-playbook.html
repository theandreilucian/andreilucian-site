Part 1 — X writing foundations

On X, good writing has to look scannable and deliver a point. Nail both and you hit your content goals without overcomplicating.

I call the readable layer aesthetic writing: spacing, rhythm, and contrast so people actually finish the post — not just fav the first line.

Rotate three kinds of value:

  • Education — frameworks, steps, proof.
  • Inspiration — empathy, milestones, transformation.
  • Entertainment — jokes, stories, sharp takes.

This playbook compresses what I use on X into four parts:

  • Part 1 — Foundations (this page): aesthetic writing + the three value types.
  • Part 2 — Rule of 3, simplicity, white space (with before/after posts).
  • Part 3 — Examples across inspire / teach / entertain.
  • Part 4 — Weekly rhythm and the skills that make every post work harder.

Steal the structure, rewrite in your voice, and ship daily reps.

Part 2 — Rhythm & clarity

Rule of 3 — Lists of three read cleaner than two or four. Commit to three beats when you list.

Example:

✓
Andrei Lucian @theandreilucian
Hype culture: • Parties • Flexing • Drama Operator culture: • Writing • Lifting • Reading Stop chasing “cool.” Start stacking skill.
✕
Andrei Lucian @theandreilucian
Hype culture: • Parties • Drama Operator culture: • Writing • Lifting • Reading • Networking Stop chasing “cool.” Same punchline — weaker rhythm.

Simplicity — Don’t use more words when fewer will do. The feed rewards tight lines.

Example:

✓
Andrei Lucian @theandreilucian
You don’t need another course. You need reps.
✕
Andrei Lucian @theandreilucian
You don’t necessarily need to buy another course if you’re struggling to see growth online — consistent practice will probably help you more than stacking more information, so this week try to prioritize execution over consumption and see how you feel.

White space & bullets

White space — Line breaks are part of the message. Short lines after gaps beat a wall of text.

Bullets — Eyes stop at line breaks. One promise up top, three bullets max in the middle, one action at the end.

White space example:

✓
Andrei Lucian @theandreilucian
You don’t need louder. You need clearer. Short beats clever. White space is part of the message. Let the lines breathe.
✕
Andrei Lucian @theandreilucian
You don’t need louder. You need clearer. Short beats clever. White space is part of the message. Let the lines breathe.

Part 3 — Inspire, teach, entertain

Mix the three tracks across the week. Inspire builds trust. Teach builds saves and DMs. Entertain builds reach and personality.

Examples:

Inspire · Teach · Entertain

Inspire
Andrei Lucian @theandreilucian
If you’re building after work, tired, inconsistent — you’re not lazy. You’re under-leveraged. One short post every day beats one “perfect” essay a month.
Teach
Andrei Lucian @theandreilucian
Posting system (steal): • Hook in ≤7 words • One idea per line • End with question or CTA • Reply to comments for 24h
Entertain
Andrei Lucian @theandreilucian
Unpopular: you’re not “shadowbanned.” You’re unclear. Make one sharp claim per week. Watch who stays.
