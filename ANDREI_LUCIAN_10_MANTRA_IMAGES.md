## Image 1 — "10 things I had to unlearn to grow on LinkedIn"  
**File:** `assets/andrei-lucian-mantra-list-1.png`

1. More posts = more reach. (Nope. Better posts do.)
2. I need to sound professional. (I need to sound like me.)
3. Nobody cares what I think. (They care if it helps.)
4. I have to post every day. (I have to show up consistently.)
5. The algorithm is the enemy. (The algorithm rewards value.)
6. I need a big following first. (I need one person who gets it.)
7. My story doesn't matter. (My story is the hook.)
8. I should copy what's working. (I should find what works for me.)
9. Engagement is luck. (Engagement is reply speed + clarity.)
10. I'm not ready yet. (I'm ready when I start.)

**Tone:** Short. Punchy. Parentheses as the twist.

- **Variation:** Rotate backgrounds (e.g. white + black text + cyan accents, or black slide + white text + cyan highlights) so the 10 images feel like a set.
