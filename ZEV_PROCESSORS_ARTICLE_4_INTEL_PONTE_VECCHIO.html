Specifications — Max 1550

Aug 18, 2024 · #GPU.

[Image: Intel Ponte Vecchio / Data Center GPU Max 1550]
Product or block diagram

Per GPU: 3D stacks; 128 Xe Cores per stack. Each Xe-Core: 8 Xe Vector Engines + 8 Xe Matrix Engines → 1,024 vector, 1,024 matrix engines total. 900 MHz base, 1.6 GHz peak. No sparsity. 128 GB HBM2e, 2.2768 TB/s. 36 Xe Links (BoD), x16 PCIe Gen5 or CXL 1.1 (BoD). 600 W max.

Nomenclature

  • 1 GPU = 2 stacks
  • 1 stack = 4 slices + HBM2e controllers + Xe Links
  • 1 slice = 16 cores
  • 1 core = 8 vector engines + 8 matrix engines
  • 1 vector engine = 512 bits
  • 1 matrix engine = 4096 bits

Stacks were formerly “tiles”; Vector Engines were formerly “EUs.”

Performance

Values measured on Aurora preproduction hardware with DGMlib. Ambiguity between vector FMA and matrix usage. Theoretical vector FMA (FP64) ≈ 14.7 TFLOPS at base clock; measured 17 TFLOPS is closer to that than to matrix peak (~210 TFLOPS theoretical).

Why Ponte Vecchio matters

Intel’s data center GPU for HPC and AI; Aurora at ALCF is the flagship. Clear hierarchy: GPU → stack → slice → core → engines. 128 GB HBM2e, 2.28 TB/s, 600 W; no sparsity; 3D packaging and Xe Links.
