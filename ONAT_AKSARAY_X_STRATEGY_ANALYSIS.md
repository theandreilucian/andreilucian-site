**Strategy:** ~69,000 tweets = extremely high posting frequency

**How to Implement:**
- Post multiple times per day (8-10 posts minimum)
- Mix of original content, replies, and engagement
- Don't wait for perfect - ship consistently
- Use scheduling tools to maintain consistency

**Why It Works:**
- Algorithm rewards consistency
- More posts = more opportunities to be seen
- Builds habit and momentum
- Increases chances of viral content

### 2. Free Value First

**Strategy:** Offers "Free X Simplified Guide" and other resources

**How to Implement:**
