Introduction

Three flagship systems: Aurora (Argonne, USA — Cray EX, Intel blades, Ponte Vecchio), ABCI 3.0 (AIST, Japan — HPE XD670, GPU nodes), Alps (CSCS, Europe — mixed Cray EX at Lugano and EPFL).

Aurora (ALCF)

Jan 30, 2026 · #usa/doe/ascr, #system. Cray EX at Argonne Leadership Computing Facility. Intel prime; Intel compute blades (not Cray). Slingshot cabinet and network.

[Image: Aurora (ALCF)]
System or cabinet

HPL: 1.012 EFLOPS (87% of system, Jun 2024); debut 585.3 PFLOPS. 10,624 nodes, 21,248 Sapphire Rapids HBM, 63,744 Ponte Vecchio, 166 cabinets.

Node

2× Xeon CPU Max 9470C (52 cores/socket, 64 GB HBM/socket), 512 GB DDR5. 6× Data Center GPU Max 1550 (128 Xe cores, 128 GB HBM2e/socket). 8× Slingshot-11. One node per blade, 64 nodes per cabinet.

Network & storage

Slingshot-11 dragonfly. 166 compute groups, 8 storage (DAOS), 1 service. Blocking factor ≈1.55. DAOS: 1,024 Coyote Pass servers (Ice Lake, Optane 200, PM1733). 230 PB, 16+2 EC, >31 TB/s.

ABCI 3.0 (AIST)

Jun 18, 2025 · #system/japan. 766-node HPE GPU supercomputer at AIST. Deployed 2024.

[Image: ABCI 3.0 (AIST)]
System or node

Top500 #15 at ISC25. Rmax 145.1 PF FP64 on 416 nodes (54%) → 33 min run. Nodes: Cray XD670, 2× Sapphire Rapids, 8× GPU per node, 8× NDR200 IB, 2,048 GiB DDR5-5600, 2× 7.68 TB NVMe, 1× HDR IB (storage). Three-level non-blocking fat tree. 75 PB Lustre + S3 on QLC SSDs.

Why these three matter

Aurora — First exaflop HPL on Cray EX with Intel prime; Sapphire Rapids HBM + Ponte Vecchio; DAOS 230 PB, 31+ TB/s. ABCI 3.0 — Japan flagship; XD670, NDR200; Top500 #15; 75 PB Lustre/S3. Alps — Heterogeneous Cray EX (MI300A, MI250X, custom GPU, Rome); reference for mixed-vendor Cray EX in Europe.
