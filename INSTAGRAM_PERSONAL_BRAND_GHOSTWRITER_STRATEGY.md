# Instagram Personal Brand Strategy — Ghostwriter (You)

**Situation:** Faceless brand → personal brand. Had 5 Reels 1M+ views (best 13M). Now ~300 views per post.  
**You:** Ghostwriter on X + LinkedIn. Want to grow personal brand on Instagram. Considering travel + “idk what to post.”  
**Reference that works:** @theandreilucian — ghostwriter, 612 posts, 5.7K followers, mix of text graphics, journey posts, motivational, lifestyle (e.g. snowboarding). Same niche, proven format.

---

## Why Your Views Dropped (And Why That’s Normal)

- **Algorithm reset:** You changed content type (faceless → face) and likely topic. Instagram treats it like a new account for that “version” of you.
- **Audience mismatch:** Old followers came for viral faceless content; they don’t necessarily want ghostwriter + growth + personal.
- **Discovery cold start:** Personal content needs a new audience. That takes 4–8 weeks of consistent, clear positioning.

**Bottom line:** 300 views at the start of a personal pivot is expected. The goal is to define who you are, what you post, and stick to it so the algorithm can find the right people.

---

## What to Post to Grow (And Eventually Go Viral Again)

### One-line position

*“Ghostwriter who turned his own story into a brand — writing, growth, and the life that comes with it.”*

So: **expertise (ghostwriting, content, leads)** + **growth/self-help** + **personal (travel, habits, “why I do X”)**. Not “random viral,” not “only travel.”

### Content pillars (stick to these)

| Pillar | What to post | Why it works |
|--------|----------------|--------------|
| **Ghostwriter / creator journey** | “My journey on X: Month 1 – 0 clients, Month 5 – $1k, Month 10 – $10k”, “How I got my first 5 clients”, “What I tell founders about content” | Proof, credibility, saves, shares. People search for “ghostwriter,” “X growth,” “content for founders.” |
| **Writing, growth & self-help** | Short lessons, one-liners, “rules,” “beliefs,” “phrases that changed how I work” | Same as your X/LinkedIn voice. Easy to turn into text Reels and carousels. |
| **You / lifestyle** | Travel, “why I train,” morning routine, coffee, workspace, one personal win or lesson | Makes you a person, not a logo. Travel is one slice of this, not the whole feed. |

**Ratio idea:** ~50% expertise + journey, ~30% growth/self-help, ~20% personal/lifestyle (including travel).

## Your Content Mix (Format by Format)

**Reels** → Travel (and lifestyle / “life of a writer”)  
**Carousels** → Writing online, personal branding, marketing  
**Images** → You, professional, in different places  

This keeps the feed clear: Reels = discovery + aspirational (travel, life), Carousels = value + saves (expertise), Images = face + credibility (you in the world).

### Reels — Travel & Lifestyle

**What to post:** Short Reels (15–45 sec) built around travel, locations, and “writer life” — so you stay discoverable and aspirational without leaving your niche.

**Ideas:**
- **Travel montage:** 3–5 clips from a trip (airport, café, view, you working) + one line: “Writing from [city]” or “This is what building a brand looks like.”
- **One place, one lesson:** “48 hours in [city]. One thing I learned about [writing / consistency / clients].”
- **Remote work flex:** Laptop in a new place each time — café, hotel, co-working — with a short tip or reflection in caption or on-screen.
- **Getting there:** Plane, train, taxi + “Off to [place]. Still shipping content. Here’s how.”
- **Trending travel audio:** Use a trending sound with your travel clips (no need to talk; text overlay with one line is enough).
- **“Why I travel and still hit deadlines”:** Quick cuts of you working in 2–3 locations + one sentence on how you do it (batch content, one day for filming, etc.).

**Hook in first 3 sec:** Show the place or you in the place. Then add the line (voice or text). Travel = hook; the line = takeaway.

---

### Carousels — Writing Online, Personal Branding, Marketing

**What to post:** 5–10 slide carousels that are save-worthy. Topic = writing online, personal branding, marketing (for founders/creators).

**Ideas:**
- “5 ways to get clients from writing online.”
- “How to position yourself as a ghostwriter (without sounding salesy).”
- “Personal branding in 10 posts: what to actually post.”
- “Writing online: 7 hooks that get reads (and DMs).”
- “Marketing yourself as a writer: what works in 2025.”
- “5 personal branding mistakes that keep you invisible.”
- “From 0 to first client with content: real timeline.”
- “How I write posts that get inbound leads.”
- “Personal brand vs. faceless brand: when to show your face.”
- “3 content pillars that actually attract clients.”

**Format:** First slide = bold hook (question or number). Last slide = CTA (“Save this” / “Follow for more” / “DM me for X”). Same font + 1–2 colours so it’s recognisable as yours.

---

### Images — You, Professional, in Different Places

**What to post:** Single images of you looking professional (not stiff) in different locations. Builds “this person is real, credible, and living the life they talk about.”

**Ideas:**
- You at a café with laptop (writing, “office for today”).
- You in a co-working space or hotel lobby.
- You in front of a recognisable landmark (city/country).
- You on a call (framed well, good light) — “Client call from [place].”
- You with a coffee + notebook — “Morning pages in [city].”
- Portrait in a new city (same style each time: clean, good light, you in focus).

**Caption:** Short. One line about the place or the lesson. Optional CTA: “Follow for writing + travel” or “Link in bio for how I work from anywhere.”

**Tip:** Shoot in batches when you travel (5–10 shots in one outfit/location). Post 1–2 per week so you always have “you in different places” in the grid.

### Daily Engagement Routine (20–30 min)

**Every day:**

1. **Reply to every comment** on your posts (within 2 hours when possible — first hour is best).
2. **Reply to every DM** (even short "Thanks!" or "More on that in the next carousel").
3. **Like + meaningful comment** on 15–20 posts/Reels from: ghostwriters, writers, founders, travel/digital nomad, personal branding. Go to Explore or search hashtags (#ghostwriter #writingonline #personalbranding #digitalnomad #remotework).
4. **Stories:** Post 5–10 slides (mix of tip, BTS, poll, "writing from [place]").

**Once per week (e.g. Sunday):**

- Review Insights: top Reel, top carousel, best time to post.

- Update bio number if you passed a milestone (e.g. "Join +900 digital creators below").

### Month 1 (Days 1–30): Foundation

**Theme:** Consistency + clarity. Let the algorithm and audience learn who you are.

**Content:**

- Week 1: Bio live (travel + epistemic researcher + ghostwriter + "Join +900…"). 3 Reels, 2 carousels, 1–2 images.
- Week 2–4: Same mix. Rotate Reel ideas (travel montage, "writing from X," "why I travel and hit deadlines," one place one lesson). Rotate carousel ideas from the doc (writing online, personal branding, marketing).
- **Batch day (e.g. Saturday):** Film 3–5 Reel clips when you're in a location; create 2 carousels in Canva so the week is ready.

**Engagement:**

- 20–30 min daily: comments, DMs, 15–20 likes/comments in niche.
- Goal: Every post has at least 1 CTA in caption ("Save this," "Follow for…," "Comment below…").

**Milestone:** By day 30 you should see 1–2 Reels or carousels with noticeably higher reach (e.g. 1K–5K views or 20+ saves). Note which hooks and topics win.

---

### Month 2 (Days 31–60): Scale + Optimize

**Theme:** Do more of what works; fix what doesn’t.

**Content:**

- Same weekly cadence (3–4 Reels, 2–3 carousels, 1–2 images, daily Stories).
- **Double down:** If "writing from [city]" Reels do best → do 2 per week. If "5 ways to get clients" carousels get most saves → do another in that style (e.g. "7 hooks that get DMs").
- Try 1–2 Reels with trending audio (travel or motivation) + your niche text overlay.
- **Batch:** Twice per month, batch a week of content so you’re never scrambling.

**Engagement:**

- Keep replying to every comment and DM.
- Add: spend 10 min in Explore, leave genuine comments on 5–10 Reels in your niche (ghostwriter, writing, travel, founders). That pulls new people to your profile.
- Optional: One collab or feature (e.g. founder you work with, another creator) — Story or Reel.

**Milestone:** Follower count moving up; one or two Reels may hit 10K–50K. Saves on carousels increase. Use Insights to name your "best format" and "best topic."

---

### Month 3 (Days 61–90): Compound + Community

**Theme:** Steady volume + real relationships. Turn engagement into a habit and a small community.

**Content:**

- Keep the same mix. Add a simple "series" in Stories if you like (e.g. "Writing from [place] Monday" or "One tip Tuesday").
- Plan 1 "hero" Reel per month: strongest hook, best visuals, clear takeaway — post at your best time and push it with Stories.
- Carousels: repeat what got most saves in Month 2, plus 1–2 new titles (e.g. "From 0 to first client," "Personal brand vs faceless").

**Engagement:**

- Reply to comments in the **first 60 minutes** after posting (biggest impact on reach).
- Build 5–10 real relationships: people who comment often or DM — reply with value, mention them in Stories sometimes, support their posts.
- Keep "Join +900 digital creators below" (or updated number) + link to website so new followers have a next step.

**Milestone:** More stable reach (e.g. 3K–10K on good Reels); saves and comments up; DMs from strangers asking about ghostwriting or your content. You’ll have a clear "best time to post" and "best content type" from Insights.
