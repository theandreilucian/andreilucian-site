Google TPUv4

Apr 11, 2025 · #GPU. See Google’s TPUv4 documentation for canonical spec.

[Image: Google TPUv4]
Processor, tray, or cube diagram

Processor

Per TPUv4: 2 TensorCores, 8 MXUs, 2 vector units, 2 scalar units; no sparsity. 32 GB HBM2 (4? stacks), 1.2 TB/s; x16 PCIe Gen3; 192 W max.

ICI

ICI = proprietary inter-chip interconnect; RDMA over P2P PCIe, host bypass; 400G each direction; reliable data layer, in-order, credit-based flow control.

Tray, cube, supercomputer

Tray = 4 processors, 2×2×1 ICI mesh. Cube = 16 trays = 64 TPUs, 4×4×4 mesh, one OCS per cube; minimum reconfig granularity. SuperPod = 64 cubes = 4,096 processors, 6,144 optical ICI links, 48 OCS. OCS (Palomar) forms job-specific torus (“slice”); reconfig in 10 seconds.

Jobs

Borg schedules jobs; Pod Manager reconfigures OCS. Topology in (4x, 4y, 4z); avoids job fragmentation. Multiple supercomputers can share a Borg cell.
