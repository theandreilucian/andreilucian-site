## 4. Pricing & when to change it

### Recommended 2026 pricing

| Phase | Price | When | Trigger |
|-------|-------|------|---------|
| **Launch** | **$47** | First 14 days OR first 50 sales | Countdown on landing (48h resets per visitor OK) |
| **Standard** | **$79** | After launch window | Email list + X post: “Price goes up Sunday” |
| **Anchor** | ~~$197~~ value | Always show | Honest stack: course + templates + bonus vault |

### Why not $97 at launch?

- Your ICP has **0–500 followers** and **low trust**. $47 = impulse band (like Dan’s trust accelerators, but tactical).
- At 50 sales × $47 = **$2,350** vs 25 sales × $97 = **$2,425** — similar revenue, **2× customers**, **2× testimonials**, **2× email proof**.
- **Stripe at $47:** you keep ~$45.34. **Gumroad at $47:** you keep ~$41.80. **~$3.54/sale saved** with Stripe.

### Price change playbook

1. **Day 0:** Email ConvertKit (1,100) + Substack (800) — “$47 for 48 hours”
2. **Day 2:** X thread + LinkedIn post — last chance
3. **Day 3:** Raise to $79 on Stripe Payment Link (create new link or use Stripe coupon expiry)
4. **Every 90 days:** One “price increase” push (Dan-style evergreen urgency)
5. **Black Friday 2026:** $47 flash for 72h (existing buyers feel smart at $79)
