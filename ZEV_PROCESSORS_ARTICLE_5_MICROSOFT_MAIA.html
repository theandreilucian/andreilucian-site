Introduction

Microsoft Maia is Azure’s in-house AI accelerator family. Maia 100 = first-gen (TSMC N5, 64 tiles, Ethernet). Maia 200 = second-gen (TSMC 3 nm, 216 GB HBM3e), optimized for inference and token generation; tile/cluster hierarchy; scale to 6,144 accelerators.

Microsoft Maia 100

Oct 22, 2024 · #GPU. First-generation AI accelerator.

[Image: Microsoft Maia 100]
Product or block diagram

Architecture: 16 clusters = 64 tiles. Tensor unit 16×R×16; L1/L2 scratchpads. Precision: 4-bit, 6-bit, 9-bit MX + FP32, BF16.

Memory: 64 GB HBM2e (4 stacks), 1.8 TB/s.

Interconnect: 12× 400 GbE per chip — 9×400G to three other Maia per node, 3×400G to T0. Each Maia to three T0 switches = three-plane. Ethernet intra- and inter-node. 4,800 Gbps AllGather/Scatter-Reduce; 1,200 Gbps Alltoall. Gather-based distributed GEMM (Hot Chips).

Process: 105B transistors, 820 mm², TSMC N5. 500 W typical, up to 700 W.

Microsoft Maia 200 — Specs and architecture

Jan 26, 2026 · #GPU. Second-gen; TSMC 3 nm, 140B transistors. Matrix: FP8/FP6/FP4. Vector: BF16/FP16/FP32. 216 GB HBM3e, 7 TB/s. 272 MB on-die SRAM (CSRAM+TSRAM). On-die NIC 1.4+1.4 TB/s. Scale to 6,144 accelerators.

[Image: Microsoft Maia 200]
Product or block diagram

Tile and cluster

Tile: TTU (matrix, FP8/FP6/FP4), TVP (SIMD, FP8/BF16/FP16/FP32), TSRAM, Tile DMA, TCP (orchestration, semaphores). Cluster: Multiple tiles, CSRAM, Cluster DMA, Cluster core; redundancy for yield.

On-chip network and interconnect

Logical planes (data vs control), QoS, hierarchical broadcast, layered DMA. Intra-node: Fully Connected Quads (FCQ). Interconnect: Ethernet + ATL (Microsoft AI Transport Layer); packet spraying, multipath, congestion-resistant flow control.

Why Maia matters

Maia 100 — First-gen Azure custom silicon; 64 tiles, 64 GB HBM2e, 12×400G, three-plane T0, gather-based GEMM; 105B tr., N5, 500–700 W. Maia 200 — Second-gen; 3 nm, 216 GB HBM3e, 272 MB SRAM; tile/cluster (TTU, TVP, DMA, TCP); FCQ, ATL, packet spraying; >10 P FP4, >5 P FP8 @ 750 W; 6,144 accelerators, 8-plane fat tree.
