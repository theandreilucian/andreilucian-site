## 📊 Tracking Tips

1. **Track RTs:** Use a spreadsheet or tool to track who RTs
2. **Follow up:** Send the DM within 24 hours
3. **Engage back:** Reply to their replies, build the relationship
4. **Ask for feedback:** After they read it, ask what they found most valuable

---

## 🔄 Repost Strategy

Post the giveaway:
- Once per week (don't spam)
- Different hook each time (test what works)
- Pin it to your profile for 1-2 weeks
- Share in relevant communities (if allowed)

---

## ✅ Checklist Before Posting

- [ ] Ebook link is ready and accessible
- [ ] DM script is copied and ready
- [ ] You have time to respond to RTs within 24 hours
- [ ] You've chosen the best hook for your audience
- [ ] You're ready to engage with everyone who RTs

---

**Remember:** The giveaway is just the start. The real value is in building relationships with the people who engage. Follow up. Help them. Build trust.

That's how you turn a giveaway into real growth.
