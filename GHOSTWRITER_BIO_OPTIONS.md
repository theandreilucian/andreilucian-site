## ANALYSIS: PROS & CONS

### ✅ PROS of Including "Ghostwriter":

1. **Immediate Clarity:** People instantly understand you write content for others
2. **SEO/Keyword:** "Ghostwriter" is a searchable term people use
3. **Established Term:** Industry-recognized title
4. **Differentiation:** Positions you in a specific category
5. **Your Original Branding:** It's what people already know you as

### ❌ CONS of Including "Ghostwriter":

1. **Limited Scope:** You do MORE than just writing (engagement, DMs, personal brand)
2. **Character Space:** Takes up valuable characters in 160-limit
3. **May Be Limiting:** People might think you ONLY write, not do full-service
4. **Your New Positioning:** You're positioning as "personal brand + inbound leads" system

### 🎯 RECOMMENDATION:

**INCLUDE "Ghostwriter" IF:**
- You want immediate clarity on what you do
- You're optimizing for search/keyword discovery
- You want to leverage your existing brand recognition

**DON'T INCLUDE IF:**
- You want to emphasize the full-service system (personal brand + content + engagement + DMs)
- You're positioning as a complete solution provider
- Character space is tight for other important elements
